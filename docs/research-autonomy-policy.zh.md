# Research autonomy: compatibility entry

The current working method is [Current research workflow](../research/README.md), especially its principles for changing the method. This page adds no separate workflow or startup checklist.

Ordinary research, implementation and same-scope recovery are delegated to the complete owner and independent peer. Directory changes, refreshed technical bindings and new invocations are not new economic hypotheses. Only affected actions are blocked by missing evidence or permission; other authorized work continues.

Apply [scientific review](research-plan-review-standard.zh.md) to material scientific changes, and actual task/device/spending/production authority to the relevant operation. Earlier generic per-card approval, mandatory eleven-row review, universal three-depth-point and whole-repository-test wording does not override the current method. Existing frozen study criteria, original evidence and accumulated consumption are preserved.

Read this compatibility entry only when resolving an older reference or instruction conflict. Historical policy text is preserved in the guidance-consolidation before snapshots under `reports/research/program/guidance-consolidation-20261015/`.
