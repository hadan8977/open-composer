### 3. The test (only after the freeze)

**Script:** `scripts/run_h20261004_pm_earnings_beat_odds.py`. Write it, and its tests on synthetic data, before running it on real data.

**Returns:** SIP daily from `data/sip/daily`, read-only, with adjusted closes. Window return = close(R) / close(S) - 1 minus SPY over the same window.

**Order inside the run:**

1. **Positive control first.** If its t is below 3, stop: write `results.json` with `verdict: "pipeline_suspect"` and report. Do not compute H-A.
2. **Calibration table.**
3. **H-A:**

   - tercile spread, gross and net (primary and stress costs);
