## Step 2: gating function plus tests (`tests/test_p2_s2_gating.py`)

`gate(eff, cols, m_eff, cash="SHY")`:
- row t (the weights effective on session t) → risky columns × m_eff[t]; the cash column gets + (1 − m_eff[t]) × the sum of the risky weights in that row.
- Append a zero cash column if absent. Its legs must exist in leg_open/leg_close; base.CASH is "SHY".
- `m_eff[t] = m_close[t−2]`: the multiplier decided at the close two sessions before (contract: state at close d effective from session d+2). For zero delay, `m_eff[t] = m_close[t−1]`.
- Before a spec's first multiplier, m = 1.

Tests:
1. m ≡ 1 reproduces `t23.simulate_effective` on the same item bit for bit.
2. m ≡ 0 holds only cash.
3. The delay alignment uses only m_close dated ≤ t−2.

**Identity check on the real panel** (stop if it fails): for each sleeve, the m ≡ 1 gated blend equals `blend_series(items, leg_open, leg_close, "O", 10.0)[0]` to ≤ 1e-12.

## Step 3: series per sleeve, spec and offset (10 bp; also 20 bp)

1. **Gated.** `gate` + `t23.simulate_effective(..., cost_bps)`.
2. **Ungated.** The frozen path.
3. **Exposure-matched volatility target** (per offset item and per window):
   - m_VT[t] = min(1, τ / σ̂[t−2]), with σ̂ = EWM(halflife 21) of the ungated item's daily net return, annualized with √252;
   - pick τ by bisection so that the window mean of m_VT equals the window mean of that spec's m_eff;
   - then `gate` with m_VT.
4. **Blend.** Equal-weight mean of the offset items' daily returns (S1, S2, S3 have 5 items, C2 has 1), as `blend_series` does.
5. **Windows:**
   - stress 2017-10-02..2023-08-31 (decision);
   - recent 2024-01-08..2026-09-17;
   - owner B window 2026-07-01..2026-09-17 (report);
   - full 2017-10-02..2026-09-17.
6. **Metrics:**

   - Sharpe differences gated − VT and gated − ungated, each with a paired stationary bootstrap 95% interval (block 21, 2000 draws, seed 20261006).
