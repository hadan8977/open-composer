# T-20261002-25 brief: realism ladder for the total-return M0/M1 long-short (is there a simple-construction book?)

Written 2026-10-02 04:30 UTC by the interactive lead (Opus).

## Why
- **v1.1 is cancelled under its stop rule.** T-24 (e218e62) admitted no new signal. The three candidates with stress-period residual IC t 2.9–5.7 (ml_pv, low MAX, near 52-week high) decayed to t 0.5–1.8 in 2024–26.
- **The construction tweaks didn't help.** T-19 (80a82a6) showed the optimised book holds TC of only about 0.41–0.51, and its P&L comes mostly from style premia. Removing the style caps changed nothing.
- **The signal-level, total-return composite looks strong.** Regime analysis `reports/research/program/regimes-2026-10-01/` (industry-demeaned, Σ|w| = 2, beta-hedged per stock with `beta_252_spy`, daily, open t+1 → open t+2, 8 bp/side) shows gross Sharpe 1.68 in A (2024-01-08..2026-06-30) and 3.06 in B.
- **Open question:** does a simple, implementable construction that keeps the style premia (rank-weighted, industry- and beta-neutral) keep most of that? Or do realistic frictions remove it: weekly rebalancing, close fills, easy-to-borrow-only shorts, real spreads and borrow? Stage 0 also found the 252-day beta hedge failing, with realised beta 0.5–0.9, so part of the signal-level number may be bull-market beta.
- **What it decides:** this cheap, local test determines whether a "simple-construction" contract (v1.2) is worth writing at all.

## Part 1 — preregistration (commit before any new number)
Write `reports/research/program/realism-ladder-2026-10-02/prereg.md` containing the rungs, variants, metrics and decision rule below. Run `date -u` in the same command that writes it, and commit it before computing anything except R0's identity reproduction.

**Variants**: composite {M0, M1} × universe {U3000, P1000}. Build the composites exactly as `AlphaSource` in `scripts/run_book_v1_stage2.py` does: themes from `alpha/book_v1/themes_<year>.parquet`, M1 weights from `FormationIC`/`formation_ic.parquet`, then rank-normal within the universe.

**Rungs**:
- **R0**: the regime-analysis M0 long-short, exactly as `scripts/analyze_equity_book_regimes.py` builds it (`year_returns`, `ls_returns`). Its A, B and stress gross and net Sharpe must reproduce `results.json` there to 1e-9. M0 U3000 only; this is the identity rung.
- **R1**: weekly rebalancing with 5 offsets. Positions are set at decision date t and held until the next rebalance; still open fills (open t+1), 8 bp/side.
- **R2**: R1 with close fills. The fill is at close t+1, so holding returns run close to close.
- **R3**: R2 with the short leg restricted to the easy-to-borrow proxy set `SE_t`, as used by `op.ShortEligibility` in the book. Short scores outside `SE_t` are set to 0 and the short leg is re-normalised to 1.
- **R4**: R3 with realistic costs.
  - Per-name half-spread from cost model v1 at the close snapshot (`open_composer/research/equity_book/costfn.py` / `costs.py` / `cost_model_v1.json`; reuse their functions), charged on |Δw| at USD 1,000,000 equity.
  - Borrow at 25 bp/yr on short notional.
  - Impact through the same cost function if it can be called vectorised; otherwise report a bounded estimate and say so.
- **R5**: R4 with portfolio-level beta neutralisation done the way the book does it. Use the EWMA-63 per-name betas (`beta_method="ewma"` in the risk model), hedge the weighted beta with SPY at each rebalance, and hold SPY in that size.
- **R6**: R5 with the VIX crash control: if FRED VIXCLS at decision date t is > 20.60, all weights are halved. Forward-fill missing values for at most 3 sessions.

**Metrics**, per rung × variant × offset and for the offset blend, in windows A, B, stress and recent (2024-01-08..2026-09-17):
- gross and net Sharpe (the long-short is self-financing, so no rf is subtracted; this matches the regime analysis);
- annualised return and vol;
- max drawdown;
- realised beta, daily OLS and Dimson (reuse `dimson_beta` from `scripts/eval_book_v1_stage2.py`);
- one-sided turnover and cost in bp/yr.

For R4–R6, also compute the evaluation-standard v2 items with `open_composer/research/scorecard_v2.py`:
- DSR, with N_eff over the four variants of that rung;
- the V4 crash statistics on the stress window;
- the recent-consistency z.

**Decision rule** (mechanical): **proceed** to a v1.2 simple-construction contract only if, for at least one variant, all of the following hold. Otherwise **stop**: the book line pauses until new signals arrive (text, etc.).
- R6 recent-window net Sharpe (offset blend) ≥ 0.90;
- R6 realised beta (OLS and Dimson) in the recent window within ±0.15;
- R6 V4 not vetoed;
- R6 recent-consistency z ≥ −2.

**Trials**: 4 variants × 6 rungs; R1–R6 are new trials. Report the running trial count for the program's DSR.
