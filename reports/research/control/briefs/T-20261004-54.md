# T-20261004-54 brief: point-in-time audit of the research snapshot (data pitfalls #62 and #63) and its effect on the v1.2 M0 evidence

Coordinator code: **R1004-T54**. Written 2026-10-04 09:36 UTC by the interactive lead (Opus).

## Why

v1.2 (M0_U3000, accounts quant1 = F1, quant2 = F2) starts paper trading 10-07. Its promotion evidence is the A1 account layer in `data/research/book_v12/` and `reports/research/iterations/t20261002_book_v12/`, built from the frozen research snapshot `data/research/equity_book/`. T-02 and T-22 found two look-ahead leaks in that snapshot. The live values are point-in-time correct, so the leaks do not block the 10-07 start. They do matter for how far the backtest evidence can be trusted, and the answer is needed before v1.2's first review (about 10-21).

- **#62: SEC daily table, final quarterly ladder.** `scripts/build_sec_xbrl_fundamentals.py` (`sue_series` at line ~769, `_ttm_events`, stage `daily`) builds each company's quarterly ladder from the final facts table. A late 10-Q, or an amendment filed at f, therefore rewrites values dated before f. It can fill a gap, change a quarter's value, or make an 8-quarter SUE window valid earlier than it really was. Examples:
  - T-02: SHPH's 10-Q filed 09-24 rewrote 67 earlier `sue_eps` rows.
  - T-22: EL, APLD, CALM and GURE also changed in non-sue columns (`eps_diluted`, `revenue`, `net_income`, `roe`, `accruals`), via facts filed after 09-17 (`reports/research/program/data-refresh-2026-10-01/live-parity.json`, `attribution.sec_changed_symbols_late_filings`). So the leak covers every ladder-derived column, not only `sue_*`.
- **#63: panel fallback mcap, full-year bucket median.** In `scripts/build_equity_book_panel.py` (stage `panel`, around line 310), `mcap_source in (adv_fallback, adv_fallback_outlier)` uses `median(close*shares/dollar_adv_21)` per adv-rank bucket of 250, over the whole calendar year, times `dollar_adv_21`. A row early in the year is scaled by a ratio that includes later months.
  - This covers about 20% of rows, and 62% of fallback rows moved when the live year grew (T-22).
  - It feeds `ep`, `bm`, `sp` and `cfp` (`live-parity.json`, `attribution.signal_columns`) and anything else that uses `mcap`.

Book-level context (lead check, `reports/research/program/data-refresh-2026-10-01/live-book-parity.json`):
- Live and snapshot v1.2 targets correlate 0.997–0.999 on five dates from 06-30 to 09-17.
- The share of the difference in fallback-mcap names is about 13%.
- Prior: the effect on M0 is small. The task is to measure it, not to assume it.
