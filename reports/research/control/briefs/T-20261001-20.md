## Method notes
- **Offset blend**: equal-weight mean of the 5 offsets' daily net, on dates where all offsets exist. Excess is over BIL.
- **DSR**:
  - Reuse `open_composer/research/campaign_statistics.deflated_sharpe_probability` if its formula matches Bailey–López de Prado 2014 (skew, kurtosis, expected max under the null from N_eff and var_SR). Otherwise implement it, and test it against the paper's worked example, if the text gives one (`reports/research/intel/sources/evaluation-standard-2026-10-01/bailey_ldp_2014_deflated_sharpe.txt`).
  - N_eff = 1 + (N − 1)(1 − mean pairwise correlation of the variants' blended daily net), as in the config.
- **Signal t**: mean / sd × √T of the signal-level daily long-short over the recent window. Also give a Newey–West t (lag 5).
- **V4 crash**: rolling 21- and 63-session sums of the blended net in the stress window. σ is the stress-window daily sd × √21 (resp. √63).
- **V5 / check e**: z = (SR_short − SR_main) / √(SE_short² + SE_main²), using the Lo (2002) iid SE. The short windows are the last 63 sessions of the evaluated data and the current phase.
- **Check b**: Sharpe over the recent-window days whose ex-ante state equals the state on the last evaluated day.
- **Check c**: for each phase in the recent window, its share of total recent net P&L. The check fails if any single phase has > 0.5 of the total while the total is positive.
- **Forward monitoring helper**: `dd_thresholds(sr, vol, sessions, n_sims=20000, seed)` returns the simulated 95% and 99% max-drawdown percentiles for a Brownian motion with that Sharpe and vol, for use by the paper engine.
