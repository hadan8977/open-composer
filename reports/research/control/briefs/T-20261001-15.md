## Part 2 — placebo and the RF1 conditions
Do this only after T-13 is `done`. Reuse T-13's functions in `scripts/eval_book_v1_stage2.py` (Dimson beta, cash-aligned series, window metrics); import them, don't copy.

### 5. Windows and Sharpe
- Windows:
  - A: 2024-01-08..2026-06-30;
  - B: 2026-07-01..2026-09-17;
  - stress: 2017-10-02..2023-08-31;
  - phases P01..P10 from `data/research/equity_book/regimes/phases.json` (`phases_recent`).
- Sharpe is the annualised daily net excess over BIL (`evaluate.window_metrics`). Compute it on the plain series and on the cash-aligned series, net − BIL + SHY.
- **Clarification (stricter reading):** per offset, take min(plain, cash-aligned), then the median over the 5 offsets. The same applies to the annualised excess returns in R2 and R7.

### 6. Conditions for the 12 variants
RF_M0_{A,B,C} and RF_M1_{A,B,C} are T-08's `M0_*` and `M1_*` close paths; do not re-run them. RF_M0V_* and RF_M0X_* come from Part 1.
- **R1**: A-window Sharpe.
- **R2**: B-window annualised net excess.
- **R3**: leave-one-phase-out. Remove each phase's sessions from the A window in turn; take the minimum over P01..P10.
- **R4**: placebo beat ratio on the A window, net and gross separately (section 7).
- **R5**: A-window beta, daily OLS and Dimson, median over offsets; both must be within ±0.15.
- **R6**: final solver failure rate, median < 0.02.
- **R7**: 2× cost replay, A-window annualised net excess > 0.
- **R8**: a downgrade flag if the stress max drawdown (median over offsets) is > 20%, or the stress Sharpe is < −0.5.
- **DSR**: 83 trials, for the A window and for the full sample 2017-10-02..2026-09-17. Report only.
- **ml_contribution_pass(u)**: RF_M0X_u A Sharpe > RF_M0_u A Sharpe, and the Newey-West t of their daily difference is ≥ 2. Use the offset-blended net series (equal-weight average of the 5 offsets) over the A window, with lag = floor(4·(T/100)^(2/9)).
- **timing_contribution(u)**: RF_M0V_u A Sharpe > RF_M0_u A Sharpe, and the stress max drawdown of M0V, rescaled to M0's stress-window volatility, is below M0's. Report only; it does not change M0V's own status.
