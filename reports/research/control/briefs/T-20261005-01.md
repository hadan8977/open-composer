## Definitions (from hypotheses.md section 3-5; restated for the code)

- Buckets at signal date t from panel `adv_rank`: L1 <= 500, L2 501-1000, L3 1001-3000, L12 <= 1000. First assert that the median `dollar_adv_21` falls from L1 to L2 to L3 (else stop: `adv_rank` direction differs from the assumption).
- Target returns, indexed by the signal date t: `e(t, j)` = stock close-to-close return from close(t+j-1) to close(t+j) minus beta x SPY's return over the same interval (beta = panel `beta_252_spy` at t, NaN -> 1, clipped to [-0.5, 3]), then demeaned within (date t, `ind_code`). Lags j = 1..22; lag 1 also split into overnight (close t -> open t+1) and intraday (open t+1 -> close t+1), each hedged with SPY's same-interval return. A lag is used only when the return date t+j <= 2026-09-17. Names without a return drop out that day (count them per day; no delisting return).
- New signals at close t: REV5 = -(close_t / close_(t-5) - 1); IREV5 = -(r5 - group_mean(r5, date, ind_code, MIN_INDUSTRY_MEMBERS)); RREV5 = -(sum of `resid` over sessions t-4..t, at least 4 finite); RREV21 = -(sum over t-20..t, at least 17 finite); ON5 = mean over t-4..t of open_d / close_(d-1) - 1; ID5 = mean over t-4..t of close_d / open_d - 1; VSHK = `dollar_adv_21_over_63` at t. Universe rows = the signal store rows at t (U3000). Report VSHK coverage of the store rows by year.
- ON5 and ID5 signs: compute their stress-window (2017-10-02..2023-08-31) L12 CIC(2..6) first, set sign = +1 if the mean is > 0 else -1, write `cache/signs.json` with the two means, signs and `fixed_at` (UTC from `datetime.now(UTC)` at write time) BEFORE any recent-window number is computed for any object, and copy it into results.json `signs`.
- Rank IC on date t within a bucket: ranks among finite values, centered and scaled; a missing value is set to 0 (neutral); IC_t = sum(z_s * z_r) / sqrt(sum(z_s^2) * sum(z_r^2)); keep the day only if at least 50 names have both values finite. Efficient form (recommended): per year, per date, build the within-bucket z matrix of all objects (n x K) and of the 24 target columns (n x 24) and take one matrix product.
- CIC(a..b) = sum over j = a..b of the window mean of IC_j. Newey-West t of CIC(2..6) uses the daily series sum_{j=2..6} IC_j(t) with 10 lags. Half-lives h_np and h_exp, and F_MOC, F_MOO, exactly as hypotheses.md section 3. Persistence: within-bucket rank correlation of s_t with s_(t-L), L = 1, 5, 21, on names present on both dates.
- MDE for CIC(2..6) at 80% power = 2.8 x (sd of the daily CIC(2..6) series x NW inflation / sqrt(n)), where NW inflation = (NW standard error / iid standard error) of that series; report it with the IC sd.
- Speed curves (L12 for all 73 objects; L1, L2, L3 additionally for the 10 candidates and m0_composite): score -> within-bucket rank-normal (inverse normal of (rank - 0.5)/n) -> `target_weights(score, ind_code)` -> `restrict_shorts(w, SE_t)` = w*_t. Partial trade w_t = (1 - kappa) w_(t-1) + kappa w*_t for kappa in {1, 0.5, 0.25, 0.1, 0.05} (no rescaling; names that leave the bucket get w* = 0); full rebalance every 5 and every 21 sessions, all offsets, report each offset and the median over offsets.
- MOC (main): the weight decided at close t is traded at close t+1 and earns e(t, 2) (close t+1 -> close t+2); the trade cost of day t+1 is sum |w_t - w_(t-1)| x (half_spread_bp(panel close_t, dollar_adv_21_t, vol_21_t, year of t+1, "close") + impact_bp(|dw| x 1e6, dollar_adv_21_t, vol_21_t) + 0.5) / 1e4; borrow = 0.0025 / 252 x short notional. Index every daily series by the signal date t; windows filter on t (last usable t = 2026-09-15). Names whose price is missing at t+1 are dropped from the position at no cost (count them).
- MOO (variant, kappa 1 and the object's best MOC speed only): traded at open t+1 with snapshot "open", earns the open(t+1) -> open(t+2) hedged return (the regimes convention).
- Metrics per configuration and window: gross Sharpe, net Sharpe, net Sharpe at 2x trading costs (same weights), turnover = mean daily sum|dw| x 252 / 2, mean gross exposure, cost bp per year, correlation of daily net returns with m0_composite's (L12, kappa 0.2, MOC). Sharpe = mean / sd x sqrt(252), no risk-free deduction.
- Best speed kappa* per object = argmax of recent L12 MOC net Sharpe over the 7 configurations.
- Composites: the 60 fast inputs (53 library + 7 new, new ones with their fixed signs) each rank-normal within U3000 at t, missing 0. FC_EW = mean. FC_WF252: IR_k(t) = mean / sd of input k's L12 lag-2 daily IC over signal dates s in [t-253, t-2] (the 252 most recent with s <= t-2; at least 200 finite, else 0); c_t = sum IR_k z_k / sum |IR_k|. FC_WF63: same with 63 (at least 50). Dates before the warm-up is complete are NaN; report each composite's first valid date. Then measure them like any object.
- Placebo (3 composites, recent window, L12, at each composite's kappa*): 20 seeds (`permute_within_date`, seeds 1..20) shuffle the composite score within each date among L12 names; rebuild weights and rerun the same speed path and costs; report the 20 net Sharpes and the rank of the real value.
- M0 blend (3 composites): monthly inverse 63-day-volatility weights between M0's (kappa 0.2) and the composite's (kappa*) daily net series; Sharpe of the blend vs M0 scaled to the blend's volatility, recent and stress.
- DSR: `deflated_sharpe(net, zeros, window=("2024-01-08","2026-09-15"), trial_count=21)` and `trial_count=193` for each composite at kappa*.
- Promotion (mechanical, hypotheses.md section 5): single new signals need recent L12 CIC(2..6) > 0 with NW t >= 2.0 and Benjamini-Hochberg q <= 0.10 across the seven (one-sided p = 1 - Phi(t)); composites: `information_ok` = CIC(2..6) > 0 and t >= 2.0; `net_ok` = information_ok and recent net Sharpe at kappa* > 0 and above all 20 placebo values. Also flag any object with stress CIC(2..6) t <= -2 as `stress_sign_flip`. `mechanical_status` is `identity_failed`, `measured_promotions` (at least one single signal or composite promoted) or `measured_no_promotion`.
