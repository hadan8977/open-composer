## Definitions (from hypotheses.md; restated for the code)

- Universe on signal date t: panel adv_rank <= 1000 (L12) and a DoltHub row at t (exact symbol); report also L1 (<= 500) and L2 (501-1000). A date-lag pair is kept only if >= 200 names have both the signal and e(t, j) finite.
- RV21(t) = sqrt(252) x sample std (ddof 1) of the 21 daily log close returns ending at close t (need >= 18 finite).
- IVRV = iv_current - RV21, sign +1. DIV21 = iv_current - iv_month_ago, DIV5 = iv_current - iv_week_ago, signs from the stress window (below). IVZ3 = ln(close_t / close_(t-3)) / (iv_current x sqrt(3/252)), sign -1. Comparator HVZ3 = same move / (RV21 x sqrt(3/252)), sign -1. Diagnostic IVRV_V = iv_current - hv_current, sign +1.
- Score = sign x signal, rank-normal within the universe at t (inverse normal of (rank - 0.5)/n). IC_j(t) = Spearman (Pearson of the rank-normal score and the ranked target) between score and e(t, j).
- CIC(a..b) = sum over j = a..b of the window mean of IC_j. Its t = `newey_west_t` of the per-date lag-sum series sum_{j=a..b} IC_j(t): 10 lags for CIC(2..6), 25 lags for CIC(2..22). Dates are the covered sessions in order (the sparse Mon/Wed/Fri segment as it is).
- Decision statistic: IVRV, DIV21 -> CIC(2..22); DIV5, IVZ3 -> CIC(2..6). One-sided p = 1 - Phi(t). BH q over the four.
- SE and MDE: SE = sd of the per-date lag-sum series x NW inflation / sqrt(n), NW inflation = NW standard error / iid standard error; MDE = 2.8 x SE. Reference effects (hypotheses section 6): IVRV 0.082, DIV21 0.096, DIV5 0.010, IVZ3 0.010; `power_flag = "underpowered"` when MDE > reference.
- Windows: recent 2024-01-08..2026-09-15 (decides); stress 2019-02-11..2023-08-31 (diagnostic, sign source); owner B 2026-07-01..2026-09-15 (report). Each lag uses only t + j <= 2026-09-17. Read nothing dated after 2026-09-17.
