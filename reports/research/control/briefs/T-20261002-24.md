# T-20261002-24 brief: residual-IC batch screen of candidate signals for book v1.1

Written 2026-10-02 04:00 UTC by the interactive lead (Opus).

## Why
Two findings say the signal set, not the construction, is the bottleneck.

1. **19:07 lead round** (`reports/research/iterations/t20261001_book_v1/residual-ic-summary.json`): the residual IC of M0 in the anchor window has t 1.16 (U3000) and 0.91 (P1000); M1 has t 2.2–2.3.
2. **T-19 post-mortem** (`reports/research/iterations/t20261001_book_v1/postmortem/results.json`, 80a82a6):
   - Removing the style caps adds nothing (D1 false).
   - Style premia carry the A-window P&L. Stock-specific P&L is +0.5–2.2%/yr in A and negative in B.

So book v1.1 needs inputs with real residual (stock-specific) predictive power. This task screens the candidates that already exist on disk, in one batch, under the same machinery as the existing themes. It does not run any backtest.

## Statistics per candidate × universe
Report:
- monthly IC series: mean, sd, t, Newey–West t (lag 2), share positive, n, for each window;
- coverage: the share of universe members with a finite score;
- the mean cross-sectional Spearman correlation with the M1 composite and with the M0 composite at the formation dates. Rebuild the composites with `AlphaSource` from `scripts/run_book_v1_stage2.py`, or from the themes file plus the M1 weights in the same way;
- **incremental residual IC**: on each formation date, regress the candidate's rank-normal score cross-sectionally on the M1 composite's score, then compute the rank IC of the residual against the residual target. Report its mean, t and NW t;
- **placebo**: 100 within-date permutations of the candidate's scores, seeds 20261002+s; report the 95th percentile of the placebo anchor t and where the real t sits.

## Admission rule (mechanical, written before any number)
A candidate is `admitted` for v1.1 only if all of these hold on U3000:
- (a) anchor residual-IC NW t ≥ the Bonferroni critical value z_{1−0.025/k}. For k = 3, 4, 5 this is 2.394, 2.498, 2.576;
- (b) stress-window mean IC ≥ 0, i.e. the same sign;
- (c) mean cross-sectional correlation with the M1 composite < 0.5;
- (d) incremental residual IC anchor NW t ≥ 2.0;
- (e) real anchor t above the placebo 95th percentile.

Report P1000 alongside, without admitting on it. Write `prereg.md` with this list and rule, and commit it before computing any candidate's IC.
