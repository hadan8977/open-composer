## Searches (harvest_search.py, channel/query/n/max-read, then what was read)

1. `exa` "low volatility idiosyncratic volatility anomaly small cap stocks
   2024 2025 out of sample" (n=8, max-read=6). 6 pages read (snippets, not
   full text -- `trafilatura` missing locally so `read direct` fell back to
   jina/exa_fetch excerpts). Best two: a 2025 Nordic-market study confirming
   the low-risk/IVOL anomaly is stronger in small-caps than large-caps but
   "largely driven by underperformance in the short legs" (a caution that
   applies to our long-only design too); a 2010-2023 US CRSP replication (UvA
   thesis) finding IVOL **positively** related to excess returns after
   removing the smallest/least-liquid/lowest-priced names -- a genuine
   negative/reversal finding for the ex-microcap US sample, consistent with
   our own local result that low-IVOL long books do not beat the universe.
   Used as confirmatory background for the coverage.json correction above,
   not turned into a new task (the local signal-card evidence is more
   directly on point and already negative).
2. `exa` "industry relative momentum residual momentum stock replication
   2024 2025 out of sample" (n=8, max-read=6). 6 pages read. Two high-score
   GitHub repos: `BrandonKKY/cross-sectional-momentum` (2024, pre-registered
   Fama-MacBeth replication of Jegadeesh-Titman on ~100 US large caps
   2000-2024, "honest null": in-sample NW t=-0.67, OOS net Sharpe +0.02, 0/5
   walk-forward windows significant -- negative evidence specifically for
   mega-cap momentum, i.e. our best-performing tier); `Dibyam-Dikhit/industry-momentum`
   and a matching Zenodo paper (Fama-French 48 industry portfolios,
   1975-2024, 12-1 vs 12-0 skip-month choice is state-dependent) -- industry
   *portfolio* momentum, not individual-stock industry-relative momentum, so
   only informs a possible skip-month refinement, not a directly executable
   design; not queued. Also read two residual-momentum academic papers
   (Blitz/Huij/Martens-style: residual momentum ~2x the Sharpe of total
   momentum, more consistent, less concentrated) -- consistent with, and
   older than, the local `resid_mom.md` result already found.
3. `exa` "merger arbitrage target stock announcement drift long only small
   cap 2024 2025 replication" (n=8, max-read=6). 6 pages read (snippets).
   Best: a 2025-10-13 working paper (`exa.ai/library/publication/k3grbvwxkr8`,
   read at abstract depth only -- full text behind the Exa library paywall,
   jev flagged `claim_only`) claiming target-firm return predictability 30
   days post-M&A-announcement from news coverage, pre-announcement volume and
   deal type, with claimed Sharpe >2.1 net of costs (not independently
   verified, methodology/universe/exact window not read). A 1992-2021 US
   study (51,780 events, ideas.repec.org, abstract only) finding small-cap
   M&A targets get outsized returns mainly **before** the announcement
   (leakage/thin-coverage effect) -- a caution that the tradable *after*-window
   may be smaller than the abstract implies. A 2021 options-replication paper
   on merger-arb risk/return (background on the classic strategy, no new
   number used). Turned into T-20260929-03 below, with the low-confidence
   caveats stated explicitly.
