## 1. What "verified" actually means on each site (read directly off the pages, not assumed)

**Collective2**: every single page, including ones for 18- and 21-year live systems, carries a blanket disclosure -- *"All performance results are hypothetical... Hypothetical performance results have certain inherent limitations."* This is boilerplate applied uniformly (almost certainly a CFTC/NFA-style disclosure about the equity-curve reconstruction method: rescaled starting capital, assumed reinvestment), **not** a statement that the trades are backtested or fake. Separately, and more informatively, strategy detail pages disclose real subscriber activity: ETF Timer shows *"Followed by live capital $102,000"*; extreme-os shows *"11 subscribers, 3 live AutoTraders, $143k following this strategy, 7,713 trades placed in real brokerage accounts."* That last figure -- real trades placed in real brokerage accounts by AutoTrade subscribers mirroring the signal -- is the actual broker-verification evidence, separate from and stronger than the "hypothetical" equity-curve label. A distinct **"C2Star Certified"** badge also exists (seen in the site's top promotional banner for a futures strategy) that appears to be a stricter tier than the default; none of the stock strategies we opened carried that badge, and we did not determine its exact criteria (FAQ blocked -- flagged as an open gap, not guessed at). The "WATCH / SIM / LIVE" text next to each leaderboard row is **not** a verification tag -- it's three subscribe-action buttons (confirmed on the detail pages: "Simulate this strategy" / "Subscribe $X/mo"), and should not be read as a live/sim status flag.

**Kinfo**: the #1 profile (Steven Dux) carries an explicit **"Verified"** badge next to his name -- Kinfo's core product is a brokerage-linked trade journal, so "verified" here means the platform pulled the trades directly from a connected broker/prop-firm feed, which is a stronger and more literal verification than C2's "hypothetical" framing. But Kinfo also carries its own blanket disclaimer ("Hypothetical performance results have inherent limitations... Kinfo makes no representation that any trading account will or is likely to achieve profits or losses like those shown") -- same legal genre as C2's, on top of the "Verified" badge, not instead of it.

## 3. The two strategies we could actually read in detail

**[ETF Timer](https://www.collective2.com/details-list/30415311)** (ETFTIMER, live since Jan 2008, 18 years): +16.1%/yr compounded, -43.9% max DD, 218 closed trades, 85% win trades, profit factor 5.0, 57% winning months. **Disclosed style**: trend-following "using proprietary technical indicators for price, volume, and relative strength," trading a rotating basket of single/double-leveraged and inverse ETPs bought outright (no margin -- "may be traded in retirement accounts since it uses no margin"). Recent trade log shows MVV, UWM, URA, FXI, IBB, KRE, WCLD, DRIP, ARKQ, EEM, SVXY, IGV, ZSL, ARKW, ETHE, FCG, SOXQ, UBT, LIT, OIH, IEO -- a genuinely diversified cross-asset ETP universe (equity, bond, commodity, crypto-proxy, volatility), not a single ticker. Manager voluntarily paused trading Dec 2020 - Aug 2022 ("during the Pandemic... to improve my proprietary technical indicators"), missing a 14% SPX gain -- an honest, disclosed miss rather than a survivorship-scrubbed record. **Year-by-year**: 2024 +18.5%, 2025 +20.7%, 2026 YTD (through 2026-09-24) +22.0% -- clears our recent-window bar on returns; -43.9% max DD (from the 2020 Q1 crash) is the same order of magnitude as our own live sleeves' worst case (S3 -56.4%). Exact entry/exit rules are **not disclosed** -- "proprietary technical indicators" is a style label, not a spec.

**[extreme-os](https://www.collective2.com/details-list/13202557)** (UyenLe, live since Feb 2005, 21 years -- the oldest record found on either site): +21.8%/yr compounded, -61.1% max DD, 5,101 closed trades (~1/trading day), 73% win trades, profit factor 1.4. Manager's own style tag: **"Short-term Reversal"** on individual US stocks (recent trades: TMUS, OKLO, VICR, WFRD, SHOP, CELH, LYFT, DY, CRM-scale names, mostly long with occasional shorts observed in the sample). 11 subscribers, 3 live AutoTraders, $143k following, **7,713 trades placed in real brokerage accounts** by subscribers -- strong broker-execution evidence for the signal feed itself. **Year-by-year**: 2024 **-12.3%**, 2025 +20.1%, 2026 YTD +6.1%. The 21-year average return is respectable, but the most recent full year on record is a loss -- by this project's own recent-window-only bar (`docs/research-mission.zh.md`), this single fact disqualifies it without any local backtesting.

## 5. Mechanisms worth a preregistered replication (3, not padded to 5)

**(a) Cross-sectional trend/momentum rotation across a diversified basket of liquid leveraged & inverse ETPs, no margin.** *Why plausible*: ETF Timer is independent, 18-year, third-party-tracked evidence for this style specifically (not single-asset timing), surviving 2008 GFC, 2020 COVID and 2022, and its 2024-2026 numbers (+18.5%/+20.7%/+22.0%) clear our recent-window bar. It is also structurally close to our own already-paper-live S1 sector-rotation sleeve (anchor Sharpe 1.36). *Tension to resolve, not ignore*: our own `dir:levered_single_asset_sma_trend_gate` already refuted single-asset SMA/trend-gating on leveraged ETFs via a calendar-shift placebo. The cheap, decisive test is exactly the reopen condition that direction already implies: rerun our existing relative-strength-ranking code (built for S1/S2) against ETF Timer's disclosed ~20-name universe (MVV/UWM/URA/FXI/IBB/KRE/WCLD/DRIP/ARKQ/EEM/SVXY/IGV/ARKW/ETHE/FCG/SOXQ/UBT/LIT/OIH/IEO/ZSL) with the same placebo suite used in H-20260918-05/H-20260919-01, instead of assuming ETF Timer's live success transfers. *Data needed*: none new -- all named tickers are in `data/sip/daily`. *Side benefit*: ETF Timer's own disclosed 2018 (-4.8%), 2020 (+18.8%), and 2022 (-5.7%) monthly figures are free, non-backtested, out-of-sample evidence for "what does a similar-style live manager do in a real bear/crash regime" -- something our own sleeves' all-bull paper window cannot yet show, and `docs/current-view.zh.md` already names this as the single biggest known risk of the live sleeves.

**(b) K-nearest-neighbor / historical-analog day-matching to predict next-day index direction, sized into bidirectional leveraged ETF exposure.** *Why plausible*: this is Adaptive Investments' disclosed **method** (not its numbers, which are already flagged in our registry as extreme and mostly inside our 2026 holdout) -- scan N years of history for days resembling today's setup, predict next-day return, size TQQQ/SQQQ/cash accordingly. KNN/analog-day regression is a real, textbook time-series technique, not proprietary magic, and it is a genuinely different model role from the LightGBM-on-price-features recipe this project has already tested and found wanting (matches the standing instruction to diversify ML method-to-layer rather than reuse one recipe). *Data needed*: none new -- QQQ/TQQQ/SQQQ daily bars on hand. *Cheap test*: implement literal KNN next-day-sign prediction on existing daily OHLCV features for QQQ, walk-forward validated, compare hit rate and a small bidirectional TQQQ/SQQQ-sized bet against buy-and-hold and against our existing model roster before any promotion-track claim -- cheap, and it tests the mechanism rather than trusting the vendor's claimed 86%/180%/33% figures.

**(c) Short-horizon (1-5 day) individual-stock price reversal, lower confidence.** *Why plausible in principle*: this is a well-known academic anomaly (Lehmann 1990, Lo-MacKinlay 1990), and extreme-os is a 21-year independent live instance of trading it on individual US stocks. *Why flagged lower-confidence rather than queued*: the same disclosed record's most recent full year (2024) was a **loss** (-12.3%) -- by this project's own recent-window-only rule this is a live, non-backtested data point suggesting the regime may not currently favor this style, not merely an untested idea. *Cheap test if pursued anyway*: cross-sectional short-horizon reversal signals are inexpensive to compute from data already on hand (`data/sip/daily`, `data/sip/minute`); if queued, it should be preregistered with the 2024 result treated as a prior, not ignored.

## 6. What to ignore, and why

- **Kinfo's "All-time Profit" leaderboard as a whole** (`https://kinfo.com/leaderboard/profit/at`, top 20 read). These are individual discretionary/day-trading humans (locations, follower counts, some joke profiles), not disclosed systematic strategies -- the platform's own filter UI offers "Must include: Equities / Options / Futures," and row 18 is literally named "Weekly OPTIONS." No annualized return, start date, or drawdown is shown on the leaderboard itself (only cumulative $ profit, win %, and avg gain % per trade) -- a structural mismatch with our long-only, systematic, 1x, stocks/ETFs-only mandate, not merely a data gap.
- **Steven Dux** (#1 on Kinfo, $11.8M all-time profit, "Verified" badge, 2,475 followers, spot-checked in detail). Real, broker-verified, but discretionary short-term momentum/reversal trading of small/mid-cap stocks mixed with call options, ticker symbols masked on the public view, no disclosed systematic rule. Independently well-known outside this platform for a style that leans on short-selling and options -- both outside our execution envelope.
- **US Stock Momentum (KDT95)** and **Adaptive Investments' own claimed numbers** -- already in our registry as, respectively, internally-inconsistent-with-its-own-audit and extreme/mostly-holdout-window. Not re-litigated here; see Section 4.
- **Growth Investment Stateg, Steady MAKE money, UnicornIndex, Stock Rating System** and similar 1-3-year, undisclosed-rule stock pickers with eye-catching returns (+62% to +130%/yr) but 40-85% max drawdowns. Collective2 hosts thousands of strategies and the leaderboard shows only current survivors of whichever ones did not blow up or get delisted -- classic multiple-testing survivorship exposure, and even if we trusted the numbers there is no disclosed mechanism to learn from.
- **Calicut Commodities, Magellan Atlas Navigator**, and other `STOCKS+FUTURES` / `STOCKS+FOREX` tagged rows -- the futures/forex leg is out of scope regardless of the stock leg's merit; not pursued further.
- **`collective2.com/grid` and `collective2.com/stock-systems`** (the actual sortable scanner, which would have given a true return-sorted top-N instead of "Popular" sort) -- Cloudflare-blocked (403 "Just a moment...") on every attempt from this box, including an 8s wait and a reload. Not worth further engineering effort against a bot-detection wall for a read-only research screen; if this matters later, it needs a different network egress or a maintained stealth browser profile, not more retries with the same fingerprint.
- **C2's own FAQ pages on "Hypothetical" vs "C2Star Certified"** -- blocked (403) via `WebFetch`; not retried via browser this pass (budget). Section 1's read is from the leaderboard/detail pages themselves, which is sufficient for this screen's purpose but leaves the exact C2Star certification criteria unresolved.
