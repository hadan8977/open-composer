 Extended hours trading sessions
 Outside of the regular U.S. exchange market hours of 9:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. ET, Schwab offers extended pre-market and after-hours trading sessions on all of our platforms. Additionally, extended+overnight (24/5) trading of all S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, and Dow 30 stocks, plus over 600 exchange-traded funds (ETFs) is available on any thinkorswim platform. That's over 1,300 stocks and ETFs to choose from.
 Pre-market session: 7:00 a.m. to 9:25 a.m.
 Orders eligible for pre-market execution must be placed between 8:05 p.m. (previous trading day) and 9:25 a.m. ET.
 After-hours session: 4:05 p.m. to 8:00 p.m.
 Orders eligible for after-hours execution must be placed between 4:05 p.m. and 8:00 p.m. ET.
 Extended + Overnight sessions: 13- or 24-hour continuous
 Orders eligible for continuous execution during extended hours (7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.) and extended+overnight hours (24 hours) can be placed at any time.
 Benefits and drawbacks of trading outside regular U.S. exchange hours
 Benefits
 Flexibility to trade on your schedule 
 Extended hours trading provides added access, flexibility, and convenience for investors to place trades outside of regular U.S. exchange hours—ideal for investors who want the freedom to trade on their own schedule. 
 Ability to react in real time to news and events 
 Extended hours trading lets investors respond immediately to late-breaking news. Whether it's earnings announcements, global market movements, or unexpected events, investors don't have to wait until the next day's open and risk missing meaningful price swings.
 Drawbacks
 Variable or lower liquidity 
 Liquidity can be limited outside regular hours, leading to wider spreads between bid (highest buyer offer) and ask (lowest seller offer) prices. All extended hours sessions may face low liquidity or sudden volume spikes, impacting execution and price stability. 
 Uncertain prices 
 During extended hours, quote prices may only reflect one trading venue, potentially differing from other quote systems for the same security. Additionally, the opening or closing of foreign markets can temporarily affect pricing. 
 Lack of calculation of index value 
 During extended hours, some index-based products may lack updates to their underlying index values, as these are typically calculated during regular trading hours.

 Can prices be more volatile when trading during extended hours?
 Because of limited trading activity during extended hours, the difference, or spread, between available buy and sell orders is likely to be greater than during regular market hours. Wider spreads, along with lower liquidity, often lead to more volatile prices and greater risk. When you place an extended hours limit order, you are ensured your limit order price or better if your order is executed, though the order may only be partially executed or not at all.
 Can I trade any stock during Schwab's extended hours trading sessions?
 Currently, most listed securities are available for extended hours trading. However, during any given extended hours session, certain securities may not trade due to a lack of trading interest.
 The overnight trading session in thinkorswim is available for many popular stocks and ETFs. You can view and monitor the securities eligible for overnight trading in thinkorswim using Watchlists. Open the Watchlists menu, select "Public," then select "24 Hour Trading" to load the symbol list.
 What's the likelihood of my extended hours order being filled?
 Limited trading activity and the number of orders placed ahead of yours may reduce the likelihood that your order will be filled. Remember, extended hours orders are executed only when there is a matching buy or sell order in the system. If there is no matching buy or sell order at your limit order price, your order will not be executed. Be aware that orders filled in an overnight trading session will be immediately visible in thinkorswim, but may be delayed in your Schwab.com transaction history.
 Can I place an order for regular exchange hours during Schwab's extended hours trading sessions?
 Yes, the extended hours session operates independently of the regular market session. Orders for the regular market can be placed at any time before, during, or after the extended hours sessions. Clients should select the appropriate order timing based on which trading session they want.
 How do I place a trade during extended hours?
 Extended hours trading sessions are completely independent from the regular trading sessions, and you must specify which type of trading session (regular or extended) you intend for your order. 
 Equity orders placed during regular hours using "Day + Extended" on Schwab.com will carry over into that day's extended hours session. Stop orders will not be eligible for execution during an extended hours session. 
 Extended hours orders must be placed during an extended hours trading session on Schwab.com, Schwab Mobile, thinkorswim, or by calling 888-245-6864 . 
 The overnight trading session is available exclusively on thinkorswim trading platforms. Log in to thinkorswim and select EXTO (extended overnight trading) to place an overnight trading session order. Only select securities qualify for an overnight session. 
 To view all securities eligible for overnight trading, open the Watchlist menu, select "Public," then select "24 Hour Trading" to load the symbol list. 
 Is it better to trade pre-market, after-hours, or overnight?
 Every extended hours trading session offers investors the opportunity to trade stocks and ETFs on their schedule. There is no clear advantage to any extended hours session, and trading outside regular market hours can introduce additional risks and volatility.
