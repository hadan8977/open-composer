NYSE opening and closing auctions

The NYSE’s deep liquidity at the open and close provides:
Superior market quality | Price discovery | Facilitation of block trading




Opening order types
 •    Limit Orders
 •    Market Orders
 •    Market on Open (MOO)
 •    Limit on Open (LOO)
 •    Opening D Order



Opening timeline
6:30 a.m.         Pillar Gateways open for order entry
                         •    Only NYSE-listed securities can participate in the opening auction
                         •    Orders can be entered and cancelled until the security is opened by the DMM, even if order entry or
                              cancelation occurs after 9:30 a.m.

8:00 a.m.           Dissemination of Opening information begins
                         •    Imbalance and Paired-off information is disseminated every 1 second for each security, if changed
                               from previous second, until the stock opens

9:30 a.m.           Opening process begins
                         •    DMMs begin to open each security
                         •     Securities able to open within 10% of the reference price can be opened algorithmically by the DMM.
                              Outside those that range must be opened manually by the DMM
                         •    Securities without crossing interest the DMM may open on a quote

Closing order types

      •     Market on Close (MOC)
                  o      Can be entered, modified or canceled until 3:50 p.m.
                  o      After 3:50 p.m. can be entered only on the contra-side of a MOC/LOC Significant Imbalance until 4:00 p.m.
                  o      After 3:50 p.m. cannot be modified or canceled

      •     Limit on Close (LOC)
                  o      Can be entered, modified or canceled until 3:50 p.m.
                  o      After 3:50 p.m. can be entered only on the contra-side of a MOC/LOC Significant Imbalance until 4:00 p.m.
                  o      After 3:50 p.m. cannot be modified or canceled


      •     Closing D Order
                  o      Limit Order Only
                  o      Will exercise discretion in the Closing Auction to a designated undisplayed price
                  o      May only be entered by a Floor broker

      •     Closing Offset Order (CO)
                   o Limit order only
                   o No validation against any imbalance publication (any size, price or side allowed)
                   o Yields to all other interest in closing auction
                   o Follows same cancellation rules as MOC/LOC
                   o Executes only against the opposite side of an imbalance; never adds to an imbalance
