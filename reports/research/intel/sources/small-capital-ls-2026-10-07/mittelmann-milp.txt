 The MIPLIB2017 Benchmark Instances (preprocessed data)
 ======================================================
 H. Mittelmann (mittelmann@asu.edu)
The benchmark instances (v1) of MIPLIB2017 have been run by a number of codes.
The following codes were run with a limit of 2 hours on an AMD Ryzen 9 5900X (12 cores, 128GB)
 COPT-8.0.3: COPT
 SCIP/spx-10.0.0: SCIP
 SCIPCO/spx-11.0.0: (SCIP-conc, parallel SCIP, forthcoming)
 HiGHS-1.15.0 HiGHS
 HiGHS-1.15.1 HiGHSp(parallel)
 Optverse-2.0.1 OPTV
 XSMOO Smoothie[FiberSCIP-cpx+HiGHS]
The instances were preprocessed by using SCIP to randomly perturb rows/columns and performing a quick presolve.
