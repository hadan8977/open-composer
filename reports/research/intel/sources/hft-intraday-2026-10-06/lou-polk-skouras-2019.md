- "We decompose the abnormal profits associated with well-known patterns in the cross-section of expected returns into their overnight and intraday components. We show that, on average, all of the abnormal returns on momentum strategies remarkably occur overnight while the abnormal profits on the other trading strategies we consider occur intraday."
- Profits are overnight or intraday with opposite signs (search summary): momentum earns overnight and reverses intraday.
