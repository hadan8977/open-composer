## Key excerpts (verbatim unless marked [paraphrase])

- "all orders submitted in paper trading will be matched against the best available current market price (NBBO)"
- "Orders are filled only when they become marketable." (non-marketable buy limit not filled until limit >= best ask; sell limit until <= best bid)
- "When orders are eligible to be filled, they will receive partial fills for a random size 10% of the time."
- "Your order quantity is not checked against the NBBO quantities. In other words, you can submit and receive a fill for an order that is much larger than the actual available liquidity."
- The page has no passage on extended-hours fills or opening/closing auction orders (fetch summary).
