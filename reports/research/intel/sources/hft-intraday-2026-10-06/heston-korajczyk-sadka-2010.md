## Key excerpts (verbatim unless marked [paraphrase])

- Abstract: "a striking pattern of return continuation at half-hour intervals that are exact multiples of a trading day, and this effect lasts for at least 40 trading days."
- Sample Jan 2001 - Dec 2005. Winners-minus-losers spread 3.01 bp at the same time next day. Authors: strategies exploiting it "lose money, after paying the bid-ask spread"; the gain over a non-daily strategy "does not cover the costs of two round-trips".
