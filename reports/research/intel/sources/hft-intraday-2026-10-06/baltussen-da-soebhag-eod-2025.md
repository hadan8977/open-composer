## Key excerpts (verbatim unless marked [paraphrase])

- Sample 1993-2019 (TAQ/CRSP): long-short intraday strategies yield "between 3.78 and 6.86 bps per day (or 9.5% and 17.3% per year)", value- vs equal-weighted, t-stats typically > 10; skip the second-to-last half hour (SLH) between signal (ROD3) and last half hour (LH).
- "Given that trading on end-of-day reversal requires frequent rebalancing, the strategy as presented might not be exploitable by many investors after accounting for transaction costs."
- Smallest 20% of firms: six-factor alpha 14.71 bp/day (t 27.20); largest 20%: 3.41 bp/day (t 10.61). Mechanism: retail contrarian buying of intraday losers and short-sellers' end-of-day risk management; "not explained by liquidity or gamma hedging".
