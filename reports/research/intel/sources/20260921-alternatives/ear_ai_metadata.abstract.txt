 We introduce <i>EAR-AI</i>, a novel scalar measure constructed using chronologically consistent large language models, to extract predictive information from earnings call text over 2009-2024. Portfolios formed on <i>EAR-AI</i>&nbsp;deliver sizable Sharpe ratios with robust out-of-sample performance. We find that <i>EAR-AI</i>&nbsp;is closely linked to firm fundamentals: it predicts earnings and revenue surprises, R&amp;D growth, capital expenditure growth, and changes in leverage. Its predictive content subsumes classical post-earnings-announcement drift measures, and its return predictability is concentrated among firms whose subsequent earnings realizations confirm its forecasts. In addition, <i>EAR-AI&nbsp;</i>shapes the expectation formation of equity analysts, and this effect is not driven by sentiment. Finally, <i>EAR-AI</i>&nbsp;captures a distinct source of return predictability relative to a comprehensive set of 125 characteristics, and combining <i>EAR-AI</i>&nbsp;with these characteristics substantially improves the mean-variance efficiency of characteristic-managed portfolios.
