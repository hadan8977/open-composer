As per MarsBit, on December 6, Alpha Arena's 1.5 season, which began on November 20, saw Grok 4.20 as the only profitable AI model with a 22.38% return, while Grok 4 recorded the worst performance at -53.39%. Elon Musk praised Grok 4.20's trading ability on social media. Other models, including GPT-5.1, GEMINI-3-PRO, and Claude-sonnet-4-5, all posted negative returns. The competition involves multiple rounds with different themes, and all models receive the same input for each round.
