Markdown Content:
Ask any model when its knowledge stops and you often get a wrong answer. Qwen maintainers have said openly that self-reported cutoff dates are unreliable because models are not trained to identify themselves. Blog posts copy each other. Aggregators lag new releases.

This guide fixes that. I fact-checked **LLM knowledge cutoff dates** across OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Qwen, Kimi, and MiniMax using official API docs, model cards, and provider system prompts first, then cross-checked against OpenRouter and aggregator data. Every row notes the source tier so you know what to trust.

If you track [AI visibility](https://metehan.ai/articles/best-ai-visibility-tools) or run [AEO](https://metehan.ai/articles/best-geo-tools/), cutoff dates matter. A model that stopped learning in January 2025 cannot reliably describe your product launch from March 2026 unless it browses or you feed it context.

**TL;DR**

*   Freshest western frontier cutoffs: **GPT-5.5** (December 1, 2025) and **Claude Fable 5 / Opus 4.8** (January 2026 reliable knowledge).
*   Biggest surprise: **Gemini 3.1 Pro** and **Gemini 3.5 Flash** both list **January 2025** in official Google docs despite May 2026 releases. Third-party blogs claiming January 2026 for 3.5 Flash are wrong.
*   Chinese labs vary: **Qwen 3.5+** uses a **2026** cutoff in DashScope system prompts; **Kimi K2.6** is **April 2025**; **MiniMax M3** is **January 2026**.
*   Never trust a model's self-reported cutoff. Check the API docs or this table.

## How we fact-checked

**Tier 1 (official):** Provider API documentation, model overview pages, HuggingFace chat templates with embedded cutoff strings, Anthropic Help Center.

**Tier 2 (secondary):** OpenRouter model metadata, aiknowledgecutoff.com, llm-bento.com, technical reviews citing vendor specs.

**Tier 3 (unverified):** Model self-reports when asked "what is your knowledge cutoff?" We excluded these from the tables unless no Tier 1 or 2 source exists.

**Two cutoff types (Anthropic only):** Anthropic publishes both a **reliable knowledge cutoff** (where knowledge is most extensive) and a **training data cutoff** (broader date range). Other providers usually publish one date.

**Release vs cutoff gap:** A model released in June 2026 can still have a January 2025 cutoff. New release does not mean fresh training data.

Checked on **June 17, 2026**.

## When cutoff dates matter for SEO and AEO

**Training data vs live answers:** ChatGPT with browsing, Gemini with Search Grounding, and Perplexity with native search can answer post-cutoff questions. The cutoff still matters for default answers, API calls without tools, and which pages get cited from memory versus retrieval.

**Brand visibility:** If your product launched after a model's cutoff and the model names a competitor instead, the fix is usually content plus retrieval, not waiting for retraining. See [how to improve brand visibility in AI search engines](https://metehan.ai/articles/how-to-improve-brand-visibility-in-ai-search-engines).

**Do not trust one run:** AI answers are non-deterministic. A model may know a pre-cutoff fact on one run and miss it on the next. Measure trends, not snapshots.

## Frequently asked questions

Here are direct, authoritative answers to frequently asked questions about large language model knowledge cutoff dates, model retraining schedules, and retrieval augmented generation:

### What are LLM knowledge cutoff dates?

LLM knowledge cutoff dates mark the point after which a model's training data ends. The model has no built-in knowledge of events, products, or news after that date unless it uses browsing, search grounding, or you provide context in the prompt.

### Which LLM has the most recent knowledge cutoff?

As of June 2026, Claude Fable 5, Claude Opus 4.8, Qwen 3.5+, and MiniMax M3 claim January 2026 or full-year 2026 cutoffs. GPT-5.5 is close behind at December 1, 2025. Gemini 3.5 Flash and 3.1 Pro remain at January 2025 despite recent releases.

### What is ChatGPT 5.5's knowledge cutoff?

GPT-5.5 has a knowledge cutoff of **December 1, 2025**, confirmed on the [official OpenAI model page](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-5.5). GPT-5.4 and GPT-5.2 use August 31, 2025.

### What is Claude Fable 5's knowledge cutoff?

Claude Fable 5 has a reliable knowledge cutoff of **January 2026** and a training data cutoff of **January 2026**, per Anthropic's models overview and Help Center. It released June 9, 2026.

### What is Gemini 3.5 Flash's knowledge cutoff?

Gemini 3.5 Flash has a knowledge cutoff of **January 2025**, per [official Google documentation](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/models/gemini-3.5-flash). Blog posts claiming January 2026 are incorrect. Use Search Grounding for recent facts.

### What is Gemini 3.1 Pro's knowledge cutoff?

Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview (`gemini-3.1-pro-preview`) has a knowledge cutoff of **January 2025**, same as the rest of the Gemini 3.x family.

### Do Qwen models know their own cutoff date?

Usually not reliably. Qwen maintainers state models are not trained for self-identification and will give inconsistent cutoff answers. Use the API model ID and provider documentation instead of asking the model.

### What is Kimi K2.6's knowledge cutoff?

Kimi K2.6 is widely reported at **April 2025** based on Moonshot release specifications cited in technical reviews. Moonshot has not published a standalone cutoff page. Original Kimi K2 snapshots (0711, 0905) use December 31, 2024 per OpenRouter.

### What is MiniMax M3's knowledge cutoff?

MiniMax M3 has a knowledge cutoff of **January 2026**, embedded in its official HuggingFace chat template. MiniMax M2.5 uses January 2025 and MiniMax M2 uses September 2025 per secondary sources.

### How often do knowledge cutoffs update?

Major western providers update roughly once or twice per year per model generation. Chinese labs release faster but publish cutoff data less consistently. A new model name does not guarantee a newer cutoff.

LLM knowledge cutoff dates decide which version of your brand ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini can actually cite. AEO and GEO work that ignores cutoffs is built on sand - if your AI citations depend on a fact the model does not know, no amount of answer engine optimization will surface it.
