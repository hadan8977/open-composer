Machine Learning at HRT

At the forefront of deep learning in finance.

 HRT AI Labs [HAIL]

For over a decade, Hudson River Trading has made a deep, sustained commitment to integrating cutting-edge AI research and infrastructure into our trading strategies. Deep learning is core to our trading, giving us a unique edge to tap into and shape the exciting innovation happening across the space. Our teams of researchers and engineers leverage the latest advances in academic research and computing to build and innovate on the most predictive automated trading systems in the world.

Meet Our Team

The nature of trading makes it an extremely challenging domain to apply machine learning. Market data contains very little signal, making it easy to overfit to noise. Our models must be robust to distributional shifts caused by a rapidly evolving world and adversarial actors who adapt to our behavior and train against our latest models. All of this must happen under ultra low-latency constraints, processing millions of bursty, irregularly spaced events in real time.

Join a Rigorous Research Culture

Hudson River Trading stands apart in the industry due to our extremely open, collaborative environment and strong common technological platform that allows us to innovate rapidly. Leveraging our substantial compute cluster outfitted with modern GPUs, and working alongside some of the best researchers and engineers in the field, you’ll push the boundary of deep learning at scale. We have a meritocratic, low-politics culture, where our researchers spend nearly all of their time on research and innovation is rewarded.

AI Researcher

We are hiring experienced AI researchers to accelerate the development of models that drive a significant fraction of HRT’s trading. Researchers work with minimal bureaucracy, strong independence, high GPU-to-researcher ratios, and excellent engineering support.
