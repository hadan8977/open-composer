Why DevEx matters to OptiverSoftware

Prove what works, improve what comes next

Work on evolving, data-rich problems at the heart of global markets, supported by systems and teams built for rapid iteration. As you build context, the challenge scales with you: shaping models, designing systems and making decisions that run live, with outcomes you can measure.

Progress you can measure
Growth comes from solving complex problems, working across disciplines with outcomes you can see in a day. Research, trading and engineering operate in a single loop, so a stronger signal or smarter execution quickly travels across regions. This environment is built so everyone, from graduates to experienced professionals, contributes directly to meaningful performance outcomes.

Intellectual challenges

As markets shift and our systems change, new research and engineering challenges emerge daily. Work in an environment where the problem keeps moving, so your thinking has to keep developing with it.

Built to learn at market scale

Shared platforms, data foundations and production workflows help ideas move from insight to application. A stronger model, cleaner release or better decision can travel across desks, systems and regions.

Peers who raise the bar

The strongest work is tested from more than one angle. Mathematicians, physicists, engineers and computer scientists build on each other’s works and challenge openly. 

Hear from employees

“So much of what I do today is rooted in the foundation I built as an intern. Through the education program, you learn options theory needed for the desk, you shadow experienced traders to see how theory applies in practice, and then you do it yourself by actually sitting on a desk and learning how to make decisions in real time.”

“Working with a team was the best part of my project work. We would have whiteboard sessions most mornings to discuss ideas, which was super fun. We also had the opportunity to share these ideas with traders during desk shadows.”

“One of the biggest selling points after my PhD was the immediate friction with reality, and that helps you iterate quickly on your research idea. You can see very fast if it works or not, and you can deliver impact rapidly.”

“The amount of freedom and transparency we get as interns is very unique within the trading industry”
