Crypto
- Quantpedia "The Seasonality of Bitcoin" (https://quantpedia.com/the-seasonality-of-bitcoin/): Gemini hourly UTC 2015-10-09..2023-06-30; buy 21:00, sell 23:00 UTC = 40.64% annualized gross, Calmar 1.79; 2022-23 max drawdown -22.7%; no costs, no validation after 2023-06.
- Alpaca crypto fees (https://docs.alpaca.markets/docs/crypto-fees): 0.15% maker / 0.25% taker at $0-100K 30-day volume, 0.10% maker / 0.20% taker at $500K-1M, down to 0/0.10% at $100M+; paper-trading applicability not stated on the page. Consequence: the frozen 10 bp per side understates crypto cost at our size; two trades per day cost about 50 bp (taker) or 30 bp (maker) per day against about 16 bp per day of gross edge (40.64%/252). Recorded as `dir:crypto_hour_of_day_seasonality_21_23utc`, not_executable.
- ACR Journal "Weekend effect in crypto momentum" (https://acr-journal.com/article/the-weekend-effect-in-crypto-momentum-does-momentum-change-when-markets-never-sleep--1514/): 2020-01-01..2025-04-30, 10 coins; BTC momentum weekend 0.0023 vs weekday 0.0012 per day (p=0.016); costs acknowledged, not quantified. Crypto-only, not tradable evidence.
- Finance Research Letters 86 (2025) "A crypto-stock weekend effect" via RePEc abstract (https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finlet/v86y2025ipes1544612325019154.html; publisher page returned 403): 20 largest cryptos (~85% of cap), Bayesian/Kalman; negative weekend returns significantly predict Monday equity declines, positive ones do nothing; holds for Nasdaq, Russell 2000, S&P sector indices; transmission strengthens markedly after May 2022 (LUNA); data 2021-01..2025-06 per the search snippet. No Sharpe, no cost, no trade rule in the abstract. This meets the reopen bar of `dir:prediction_market_crypto_as_equity_signal` ("peer-reviewed study of a specific tradable lead-lag") on paper, tradability unknown. -> queued.

Not usable as data for us; noted only. Key caution taken from it: the Monday gap already contains most weekend information.

Small-cap short drift and dilution
- Kargarzadeh et al., arXiv 2608.14014 (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.14014, PDF read locally, Sections 5.2, 5.6, Table 6): 4.57M news articles, 2,591 tradeable symbols, 2023-2026, beta-adjusted vs SPY with rolling 252-day beta. Neutral-news stocks trail the beta benchmark by -0.92%/month (small caps), -0.58% (mid), -0.34% (large); quiet stock-days -0.74%/-0.88%/-0.59%. Table 6 (positions close day +5 to +20, beta-hedged): fade small-cap launch/partnership 15.8% ann. gross, Sharpe 1.35, 1.20/1.06/0.77 at 5/10/20 bp per side; "short any covered small cap" 15.9%, Sharpe 1.35, 1.21/1.07/0.78; the two curves lie on top of each other, "the news direction adds nothing"; following legal/regulatory news 4.1%, Sharpe 0.66, negative at 20 bp. Borrow fees and locates not modelled. Two reads: (1) further negative evidence for news-direction shorts (agrees with T-20); (2) a 2023-2026 unconditional short-small-cap drift with Sharpe about 1.35 gross that is also the placebo every short-side test must beat. -> queued as `dir:small_cap_background_drift_short_basket`.
- QuantRocket "Is There Alpha in Borrow Fees?" (https://www.quantrocket.com/blog/borrow-fees-alpha): US stocks ex-microcaps, IB borrow archive 2018-04..2024-04; top-decile borrow-fee shorts lose money gross of fees but fees "eat up" the profit, net about flat. Verdad "Costly Shorts" (https://verdadcap.com/archive/costly-shorts): HTB stocks negative over 3/6/12 months 2019-2023, no net-of-fee number, "most pragmatic" use is avoiding longs.
