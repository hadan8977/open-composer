# H-20260923-11 S3 bear-regime gate vs an exposure-matched control

- Status: **preregistered, not executed** (round 1) -- hypothesis family: levered_rotation_risk_overlay
  -- layer: sizing/risk overlay on top of a frozen book (overlay itself not re-tested) -- data tier:
  **tier 3** (OHLCV-derived quantities only: price, moving averages, a credit-proxy ETF ratio, and
  the book's own simulated equity curve)
- Previous link: H-20260922-02 (S3 leveraged rotation plus a volatility target and drawdown brake:
  vol-target 40% passed G1-G5; the drawdown brake added no increment and lost to its own
  calendar-shift placebo in 70% of seeds -- reopen_if "a drawdown signal validated with an
  exposure-matched control", registry row `dir:drawdown_brake_overlay`)
- Iteration dossier: `reports/research/iterations/h20260923_11_s3_bear_guard/` (direction-review,
  external-brief, candidate-manifest with 5 candidates, search-space, cost-contract,
  data-feasibility; passed `oc research direction-check` and

- One-line hypothesis: does a bear-regime gate on S3 (move S3's risk weight to SHY while the gate
  is on) cut the pre-window (2016-2023) drawdown by more than an exposure-matched lower volatility
  target would, without breaking the frozen anchor-window return bar?

## Why

New evidence justifies reopening a family the project had otherwise closed. Commit 01df33d
(`reports/paper/renewal/2026-09-23-s3-stress-2016-2023.md`, `scripts/stress_s3_pre_window.py`)
replayed the exact frozen, already-renewed S3 book (vt40 + QQQ-oversold dip boost) on
2016-01-04..2023-09-15 -- a window that precedes every window ever used to select or renew S3 --
and found CAGR 30.7%, Sharpe 0.83, max drawdown -48.0% (2018-01-26..2019-08-05), with losing years
2018 (-22.0%) and 2022 (-33.6%). Every earlier bear-guard test on S3 used only 2023-09..2026-09, a
window with no bear market, so it could not show whether a guard has value; that is the
identifiable new information this round is built on.

The mechanism is not invented here. Gayed and Bilello (2016, SSRN 2741701, "Leverage for the Long
Run") show that leveraging while the broad market is above its Moving Average and deleveraging to
Treasury bills when it is below beats buy-and-hold and constant leverage, robust across Moving
Average windows and market cycles -- the published giant behind BG01/BG02/BG04. Faber
(2007/2013, SSRN 962461) is a second, independent giant for the same class across unlevered asset
classes. Gehrman's r/LETFs live, real-money track record (Sep 2026 update, running since March
2024) is an independent post-publication replication of the identical 200-day-SMA/cash rule on a
related instrument (SSO), confirming it is a real position someone holds today, not only a
backtest curiosity -- though it cannot show pre-2024 behavior.

Negative evidence bounds this round tightly. `dir:drawdown_brake_overlay` (refuted): an
equity-curve drawdown brake added no increment over the vol target and lost to its own
calendar-shift placebo in 70% of seeds; its own `reopen_if` is "a drawdown signal validated with
an exposure-matched control" -- this round's EM-k implements exactly that. H-20260922-02 (same
book): confirms the vol-target-only book, not a brake, is what actually passed G1-G5.
`dir:levered_single_asset_sma_trend_gate` (refuted): a 200-session SMA gate used to *select* which
single leveraged ETF to hold lost to a calendar-shift placebo in 100% of seeds -- a different
failure mode (asset selection, not de-risking an already-chosen book) but the same placebo
discipline is required and tightened here, because a slow 100/200-session gate barely changes
state over the original 1-20 session shift. `dir:letf_multi_signal_regime_vote` (harvested): the
LRS Sentinel author's own start-year table shows a headline 49.9% CAGR is a post-2013 artifact
(2001 start: 17.7%/-72.5% drawdown) -- why this round's design window starts in 2016, before the
select window, rather than picking a favorable start. `dir:macro_credit_drawdown_probability_gate`
(harvested, not executable as described): proposed a credit/macro gate but never disclosed its
indicator set; BG03 (HYG/IEF ratio) is this round's own fully-specified, executable credit-based
alternative. `docs/handoff-2026-09-23.zh.md` P3: the decisive control for any drawdown/regime
signal is an exposure-matched comparison, which is what EM-k is.

## Design

Machine (frozen, not searched, copied unchanged from the live S3 vt40+boost renewal): A4 menu
(TQQQ, SOXL, UPRO, USD, TECL), 63-session momentum lookback, top 2, monthly rebalance, absolute
momentum filter vs SHY, 21-session realized-volatility target (base 0.40, dip-boost 0.60 for 10
sessions after QQQ closes above its 200-session SMA with Wilder RSI(10) below 30), leverage cap
1.0, cash/fallback SHY. Signal on a completed close, fill at the next open. Cost model 10 bp per
side primary / 20 bp per side stress, `sum(|delta w|) x slippage`, reused contract id
`cost_v1_10_20bps_next_open`. Data: local SIP daily adjusted archive; risk-free BIL.

Candidates (5, exactly as specified, each an independent comparison, no selection among them):
- **BG00** reference: S3 as renewed, no gate.
- **BG01**: QQQ close < its own 200-session SMA.
- **BG02**: SMH close < its own 200-session SMA (semis-specific version).
- **BG03**: HYG/IEF price ratio < its own 100-session SMA (credit-trend gate).
- **BG04**: the unscaled S3 book's own equity curve < its own 200-session SMA (equity-curve
  trend; the unscaled curve is the same picks/lookback/top-2/absolute-momentum book with no vol
  target and no boost, i.e. the `s3_no_overlay` series already computed by
  `scripts/stress_s3_pre_window.py`).

For every gate: while ON, all of S3's non-cash risk weight moves to SHY; gate state is checked
every session (not only on S3's monthly rebalance or vol/boost recompute days), signal on a
completed close applied at the next open.

Controls (preregistered, not candidates): for each gate k, **EM-k** is S3 with both the base
(0.40) and boost (0.60) volatility targets scaled by one factor c in (0, 1], found by bisection on
the design window only, so EM-k's mean daily gross risk exposure (sum of non-cash weights after
the multiplier) equals gate k's mean daily gross risk exposure over the design window -- no gate
signal, continuously scaled. **CS-k** is 20 placebos per gate: gate k's own design-window daily
on/off series, circularly shifted by `offset_j = round(N * j / 21)` sessions for j = 1..20 (N =
design-window session count), which preserves on/off duration and time-in-market but breaks
alignment with actual market states (upgraded from a short 1-20 session shift, which is adequate
for a fast signal like the earlier 25% drawdown brake but not for a slow 100/200-session trend
gate).

Windows: design window 2016-01-04..2023-09-15 (warmup requested from 2015-01-02; the local SIP
daily archive's earliest bar for every symbol needed -- QQQ, SMH, HYG, IEF, SHY, BIL, and the five
S3 menu ETFs -- is actually 2016-01-04, so no true pre-window warmup exists). S3 itself first
holds risk 2016-05-02 (63-session lookback warmup); BG01/BG02's 200-session SMAs and the dip boost
are first valid 2016-10-17; BG03's 100-session HYG/IEF ratio SMA is first valid around 2016-05;
BG04's 200-session equity-curve SMA is first valid around 2017-02 (200 sessions after S3 first
holds risk). Before its valid date, each gate reads as off, identical to BG00 -- a warmup
limitation, not a lookahead bias -- and it precedes, so does not compromise, the 2018 and 2022
drawdown episodes the window exists to test. Frozen anchor 2024-01-08..2026-09-16 is a do-no-harm
check only, at 10 and 20 bp. The 2026 holdout (2026-01-02..2026-09-17) was already spent
evaluating the live S3 book at its 2026-09-23 renewal, so **no holdout claim is made for any gate
in this round**.

## Gates (adoption rule)

All must hold, per gate, independently, for a gate to be recommended:
- **(a)** design-window max drawdown improves by >= 10 percentage points vs BG00.
- **(b)** design-window Sharpe >= BG00 Sharpe + 0.10.
- **(c)** design-window Sharpe > its EM-k control's Sharpe.
- **(d)** design-window Sharpe beats >= 18 of its 20 CS-k placebos.
- **(e)** anchor-window (do-no-harm only) at both 10 and 20 bp: CAGR >= 50% and max drawdown
  >= -35% (the frozen gate G1).

Multiple comparisons: 4 gates (BG01-BG04); all four are reported in full regardless of outcome. If
no gate passes all of (a)-(e), the verdict is **"no guard; keep the 25% exit"**.

## Placebos

CS-k circular-shift placebos, as defined above under Design/Controls: 20 per gate, 80 total,
design window only, criterion (d).

## Stop condition

No gate passes all of (a)-(e) -> the direction is refuted for this mechanism; reopen only with a
new gate mechanism (for example a model that forecasts regime transitions rather than reacting to
a crossed moving average) or new, fully-disclosed data (for example a named, replicable
macro/credit indicator set, unlike the still-unspecified `dir:macro_credit_drawdown_probability_gate`
post), not by re-tuning these four gates' windows, SMA lengths, or thresholds, and not by widening
the candidate list. Search budget for the decisive test: 88 simulations (4 gates + 4 EM-k controls
+ 80 CS-k placebos), fixed; BG00 needs no new simulation. This is round 1.

## Results

Executed 2026-09-23 (`scripts/run_h20260923_11_s3_bear_guard.py`; report
`reports/research/iterations/h20260923_11_s3_bear_guard/report.md`). **Refuted: no gate passes;
keep the 25% exit.** BG00 reproduces the stress replay exactly (30.7% / 0.83 / -48.0%) and the
frozen anchor (116.3% / -32.3%). Best gate BG02 (SMH < SMA200): 30.9% / 0.85 / -41.1%, beats its
exposure match (0.81) but only 17 of 20 circular shifts and misses (a) and (b). BG01 makes the
drawdown worse (-61.1%); BG03 collapses (6.9% / 0.34); BG04 cuts the drawdown to -42.6% at a lower
Sharpe (0.80). All four keep anchor G1 at 10 and 20 bp. Lesson: slow price-trend gates on a book
that already carries a volatility target and an absolute-momentum filter re-time the same
information late; the exposure they remove costs about as much as the drawdown they save.
Reopen only with a new mechanism (a regime-transition forecast validated against an exposure
match) or new disclosed data, not by re-tuning these windows.
