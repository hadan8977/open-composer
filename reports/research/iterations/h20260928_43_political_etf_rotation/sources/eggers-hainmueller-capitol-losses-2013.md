# Snapshot: Eggers & Hainmueller 2013 -- "Capitol Losses"

URL: https://andy.egge.rs/papers/EggMueller_capitol_losses_jop_2013.pdf
Journal of Politics, 2013. Located via WebSearch ("congressional trading no
abnormal returns Eggers Hainmueller 2013 study"). Fetched: 2026-09-29T01:53:00+00:00

## Method
Reconstructed actual portfolios of US Congress members from financial
disclosure forms (2004-2008); transaction-based panel regressions using the
Fama-French 3-factor and Carhart 4-factor models (a break from prior
synthetic-portfolio studies).

## Finding
No evidence of either informed trading or above-market portfolio returns for
Congress as a whole or any identifiable subset of members. Congressional
trades in 2004-2008 under-performed a passive index fund by 2-3%/yr. The
paper argues widespread congressional "insider trading" is more myth than
reality, contrasting earlier studies (e.g. Ziobrowski et al.) that had
claimed abnormal returns.
