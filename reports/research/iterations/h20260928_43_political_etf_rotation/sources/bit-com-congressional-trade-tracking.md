## STOCK Act & Disclosure Rules
Members of Congress must publicly disclose securities transactions exceeding
$1,000 within 30-45 days. The disclosure includes the asset, transaction
type, and value range -- but not exact prices or reasoning.

## Copy Trading Phenomenon
Platforms like Quiver Quantitative and Capitol Trades aggregate these
filings, enabling retail investors to replicate lawmakers' trades. Nancy
Pelosi's portfolio notably returned 71% in 2024, significantly outpacing the
S&P 500's 25% return.

## NANC ETF
Described as a regulated ETF (Unusual Whales Subversive Democratic ETF,
managed by Tidal Financial Group) that explicitly mirrors trading activity of
Democratic congressional members and is available for retail trading.

## Critical caveat on information lag
"By the time a disclosure appears on Quiver Quantitative, the original trade
may have been placed over a month earlier, and the market may have already
partly priced in whatever information advantage the lawmaker held."
