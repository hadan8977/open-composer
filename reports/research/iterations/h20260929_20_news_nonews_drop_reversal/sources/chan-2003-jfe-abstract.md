## Claim (abstract level)

Using a comprehensive headline database to classify individual-stock price moves as accompanied by identifiable public news or not:

- Stocks with **bad public news** show a significant **negative drift** for up to 12 months after the news (investors underreact / react slowly to bad news).
- Stocks with a **large price move and NO identifiable news** tend to **reverse** the following month (investors overreact to a price move with no fundamental news attached).
- Both results hold after controlling for size and book-to-market.
- Robustness checks show the no-news reversal is **concentrated in smaller, more illiquid stocks** and weakens (but does not vanish) once low-priced stocks are excluded.

## Limitations for this iteration

- Original sample predates 2000; no post-2015 magnitude figure in the abstract.
- "No news" here means "no identifiable headline in Chan's news database", not literally zero information (a mechanism this iteration's news_packets + 8-K-item-2.02 classification approximates, not reproduces exactly).
