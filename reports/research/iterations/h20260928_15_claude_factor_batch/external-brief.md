# External Brief: h20260928_15_claude_factor_batch

Nine primary sources opened and quote/abstract-verified (see external-brief.json
and the source cards jsonl for full fields): three on LLM-driven formulaic
alpha mining with disclosed out-of-sample windows (AlphaAgent arXiv:2502.16789,
R&D-Agent(Q) arXiv:2505.15155, Alpha-GPT arXiv:2308.00016), and six academic
papers grounding the eight preregistered factor hypotheses (Cohen/Malloy/
Pomorski 2012 on opportunistic insiders, Gervais/Kaniel/Mingelgrin 2001 on the
high-volume return premium, Christophe/Ferri/Hsieh 2010 and Diether/Lee/Werner
2009 on informed short selling, Chordia/Subrahmanyam/Anshuman 2001 on trading-
activity variability, Ke/Huddart/Petroni 2003 on insider trading around
earnings). The two headline findings: AlphaAgent's own U.S. S&P 500 test IC
(0.0056) is about a quarter of its China CSI 500 IC (0.0212) for the identical
method, setting a realistic, not-optimistic bar; and R&D-Agent(Q)'s NASDAQ 100
post-2023 OOS test shows a genuine ~3.5-4x IC edge over classical factor
libraries (Alpha158/360/AutoAlpha), the closest existing precedent to this
task's own decision rule. See direction-review.json for the full prior-art
comparison against the registry (dir:llm_hypothesis_code_backtest_loop already
adopted, dir:llm_formulaic_alpha_mining_price_volume not_executable and not
what this task does, HARLA and the EMNLP loop paper already read and folded
in) and reports/research/signal-cards/index.jsonl (24 existing rank cards,
checked before designing every factor to avoid re-proposing a refuted
mechanism without a new element).
