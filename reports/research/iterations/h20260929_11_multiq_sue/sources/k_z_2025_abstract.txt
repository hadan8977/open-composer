Kaczmarek & Zaremba (2025), Finance Research Letters vol. 86 (PE), DOI 10.1016/j.frl.2025.108751. Abstract (via ScienceDirect abs page / RePEc / search summaries, fetched 2026-09-29): ML (elastic net) extracts information from multiple quarters of standardized unexpected earnings (SUE). Accounting for earnings patterns from the distant past substantially improves return forecasts: Sharpe ratios nearly double; alphas remain significant after controlling for one-quarter SUE and streak effects. Models using up to 12 quarters outperform shorter-horizon and streak-based approaches in returns, Sharpe ratios and alphas. Gains are especially strong among large-cap stocks, where the latest surprises are quickly priced in but older ones remain overlooked.
