Signal: at the close of each month's final trading day (D), trailing 252-trading-day total return of each sector ETF.

Leg 2 (D+2, D+3): reverse position (flip signal). Strat2 reversed: 3.26% ann, 0.46 Sharpe.
Leg 3 (D-10 to D-5): enter the momentum position using the trailing 252-day ranking computed at the most recent month-end. Strat2: 1.35% ann, 0.32 Sharpe.
Variants: Strat1-SPY (market-neutral): long top-3 + short SPY equal notional. Strat2 (long-short): long top-3 + short bottom-3 equal notional.
Composite: Strat2 5.99% ann / 0.55 Sharpe; Strat1-SPY 3.77% ann / 0.54 Sharpe; invested ~40% of trading days.
