Mitchell and Pulvino (Journal of Finance, December 2001) analyze 4,750 mergers 1963-1998. 'After the announcement of a merger or acquisition, the target company's stock typically trades at a discount to the price offered by the acquiring company.' Risk arbitrage returns show low correlation with the market in normal periods and rise in downturns (similar to selling uncovered index puts). Transaction costs reduce reported returns compared with other studies; after controlling for the non-linear payoff and costs, excess return about 4% per year (search-result summary, same session).
