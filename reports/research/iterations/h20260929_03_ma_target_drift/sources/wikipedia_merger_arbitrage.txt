Fetched 2026-09-29 (WebFetch page summary).
Target trades below the offer price because of completion risk. Break rate 8.0% across 2,182 mergers (1990-2007) in one analysis; 22.7% for 1,901 mergers 1981-1996 (Baker-Savasoglu); hostile deals succeed 38%, friendly 82%. Reported excess returns: Baker-Savasoglu 9.6% annualized, Mitchell-Pulvino 6.2% annualized. Transaction costs significantly erode these returns.
