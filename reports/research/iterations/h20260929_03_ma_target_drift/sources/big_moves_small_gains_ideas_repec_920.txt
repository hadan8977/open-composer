Big Moves, Small Gains: Unpacking the Size Effect in Takeovers. 51,000+ US events 1992-2021. Small stocks are often the targets of transactions that significantly enhance their returns; before deals are announced, smaller companies experience disproportionately large gains, likely because less analyst coverage leaves the deal news largely unanticipated.
