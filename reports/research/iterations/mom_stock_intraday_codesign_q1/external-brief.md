# External Brief: mom_stock_intraday_codesign_q1

The incremental review combined foundational momentum research, 2023-2026 ML and LLM work, official SEC and Alpaca documentation, and current open-source project behavior. It does not treat GitHub popularity, provider marketing, or a new preprint as Alpha evidence.

The evidence supports long-only medium-horizon stock momentum, industry-relative variants, and explicit cost control as the most realistic personal-account paths. It supports intraday research only when opening, time-slot, overnight, continuation, and reversal hypotheses remain separate. IEX bars are research evidence rather than SIP execution evidence.

ML is redesigned around grouped return ranking, downside prediction, market context, and one fixed cost-aware portfolio mapping. Deep sequence, graph, reinforcement-learning, and end-to-end order models are deferred until a wider PIT panel and richer execution data exist. LLM work is limited to grounded event extraction and narrative change with immutable versions, rights metadata, and forward-only evidence when first-seen history is unavailable.

All source claims, dates, applicability, and limitations are stored in `external-brief.json` and `reports/harness/source_cards/us_stock_intraday_momentum_codesign_q1.jsonl`.
