Cboe S&P 500 Putwrite Index ((PUT))

Index Description
The Cboe S&P 500 PutWrite Index (PUT) tracks the value of a passive investment strategy
("Cboe S&P 500 Collateralized Put Strategy") which consists of overlaying S&P 500 (SPX)
short put options over a Treasury bills account invested in one-month and three-month Treasury
bills. The SPX puts are struck at-the-money and are sold on a monthly basis, usually on the 3rd
Friday of the month.

Index Highlights
EQUITY EXPOSURE - The PUT Index simulates a strategy for money market accounts to gain
access to the equity market without holding stocks. Instead, the index overlays SPX short put
options over a Treasury bills account.
LOWER BETA - The Index has generally followed the trend of the S&P 500 Index, with a beta
lower than 1.
LESS RISK - The PUT Index had lower long-term volatility and less maximum drawdown than
the S&P 500 Index.
