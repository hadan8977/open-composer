# External Brief: mom_multiasset_ai_r2

The reviewed literature and production methodologies support a factor-library-plus-ranking design, not blind expansion of the previous two boosted regressors. The round therefore introduces fold-local factor screening, LambdaRank, deterministic/ML blending, regime features and ensemble disagreement controls.

The LLM contribution is deliberately upstream: it compiles source-backed formulas into a static registry with hashes. No generated text or live model response participates in a trading decision. Historical tail data is already exposed by the previous round and is labeled as a challenge set rather than a pristine lockbox.
