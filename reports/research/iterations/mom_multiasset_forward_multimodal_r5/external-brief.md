# External Brief: mom_multiasset_forward_multimodal_r5

## Objective

Evaluate a matched 14-ETF family containing deterministic rules, trained models, frozen LLM-derived formula factors, a combined model, an exact missing-modality fallback, and a shuffled placebo. Historical results are transfer evidence, not final paper authorization.

## Evidence Boundary

- The source set contains six method papers, official SEC documentation, and current Alpaca data/order/paper documentation.
- The preregistered scout added FinBench only as support for the already-frozen time-gated Brier calibration rule; it did not add a candidate or threshold.
- R5 uses no live LLM inference, news, filings, or transcript packets.
- Four formula factors are transferred unchanged from R2 and cannot be regenerated after outcomes are known.
- Every candidate uses the same universe, daily SIP panel, next-session-open timing, 10 bps primary costs, chronological folds, and benchmark family.

## Candidate Revision

The family is frozen at eight candidates: two deterministic controls, two fold-local trained models, formula-only LLM participation, a combined model, an exact missing-modality fallback, and a seeded shuffled placebo. No ninth candidate may be added after evaluation begins.

## Promotion Boundary

No historical result alone authorizes Alpaca Paper. A candidate must pass matched research gates, start a new forward epoch on or after 2026-07-30, complete the configured forward and TCA gates, pass execution/safety review, and receive explicit paper-only confirmation.
