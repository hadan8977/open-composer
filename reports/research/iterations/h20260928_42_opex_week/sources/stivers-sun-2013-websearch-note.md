## Finding (per WebSearch summary of the ScienceDirect abstract)
For S&P 100 stocks, weekly returns over the option-expiration (OE) week (the
month's 3rd-Friday week) are high relative to (1) the same week's returns
for stocks with less option activity, (2) the same stock's own other weekly
returns, (3) risk-adjusted (asset-pricing) alphas. The following (4th-Friday)
week's returns are modestly weaker. Mechanism: reduced call open interest
during the OE week implies a reduced market-maker net long-call position,
hence reduced short-stock delta-hedge positions -- consistent with
Quantpedia's summary and independently supports the option-expiration-week
tailwind mechanism. Declining option-implied-volatility risk perceptions
over the OE week are also cited as a contributing factor.
