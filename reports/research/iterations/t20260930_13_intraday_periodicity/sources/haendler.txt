Korajczyk , Northwestern University - Kellogg School of Management Follow Ronnie Sadka , Boston College - Carroll School of Management Follow Publication Date 11-14-2025 Abstract Heston et al. (2010) find puzzling intra-day patterns in stock returns, which are particularly strong at the beginning and end of the trading day. We demonstrate the patterns persist out-of-sample and test several explanatory variables, including trading frictions and proxies for trader type. They jointly explain all the open and midday periodicity, and up to 30% of the closing interval periodicity. Our proxies for institutional trading-changes in lendable shares, VWAP-like trading, and index inclusion-are the most significant explanatory variables. Overall, the results suggest that open periodicity is driven by VWAP trading while close periodicity by market-on-close trading.
