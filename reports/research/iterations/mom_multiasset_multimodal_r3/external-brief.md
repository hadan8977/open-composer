# External Brief: mom_multiasset_multimodal_r3

The source review changes the next round from another price-factor expansion into a company-information and model-role experiment. The deterministic 12-1 strategy remains champion. SEC filings, earnings releases and timestamped news are candidate information modalities; licensed earnings-call text is optional and blocked when provenance or redistribution rights are absent.

LLM output is restricted to grounded structured extraction with evidence spans. Historical LLM transformations are retrospective because the current model may contain later world knowledge. Formal LLM contribution therefore requires packets frozen after the 2026-07-15 epoch. Machine learning is evaluated as ranking, risk, disagreement/meta-gating and cost-aware shrinkage, each against matched deterministic, missing-modality and placebo controls.

The knowledge layer reuses fresh prior claims and negative experiments, refreshes stale or conflicting claims, and scouts genuinely new sources. It partitions public literature, train-only empirical outcomes, challenge results and forward observations so research memory cannot leak a holdout verdict back into candidate generation.
