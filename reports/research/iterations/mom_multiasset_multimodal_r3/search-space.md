# Search Space: mom_multiasset_multimodal_r3

The round is capped at 24 preregistered candidates: six champion/text ablations, six catalyst/risk roles, six regime/disagreement roles and six cost-aware shrinkage variants. No model family or threshold may be added after challenge results are visible.

All candidates use the same decision dates, source universe and 5/10/20 bps cost table. The benchmark family includes same-symbol buy-and-hold, equal-weight universe, SPY market proxy, XLK sector/theme proxy, BIL cash proxy and the ex-post best available symbol. Text variants must include missing-modality, shuffled-text and stale-text controls. Historical retrospective LLM outputs cannot produce a formal LLM pass; only the frozen forward epoch can do so.
