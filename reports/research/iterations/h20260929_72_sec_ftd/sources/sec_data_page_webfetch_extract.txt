Source: https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/fails-deliver-data
Fetched: 2026-09-29 via the WebFetch tool (page summary extract; a raw curl snapshot returned HTTP 403 Request Rate Threshold Exceeded).
File format: pipe delimited text files.
Fields: Settlement Date (8 digits); CUSIP (9 characters); Ticker Symbol (10 characters); Quantity/Fails (unlimited digits); Company Description (30 characters); Previous Day Closing Price (unlimited, or "." if unavailable or under one penny).
Publication schedule: first half of each month available at month's end; second half around the 15th of the following month; prior to July 2009 organized by calendar quarter.
Reporting threshold: before September 16, 2008 only securities with fails-to-deliver balances of at least 10,000 shares; from September 16, 2008 all securities with fails-to-deliver balances, zero-balance records excluded.
SEC caveat (verbatim): "fails-to-deliver can occur for a number of reasons on both long and short sales. Therefore, fails-to-deliver are not necessarily the result of short selling, and are not evidence of abusive short selling or 'naked' short selling."
Figures are aggregate balances at specific points in time, not daily transaction amounts.
