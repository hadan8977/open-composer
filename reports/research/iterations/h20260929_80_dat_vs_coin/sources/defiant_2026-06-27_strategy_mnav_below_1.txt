Strategy's enterprise market-to-NAV (mNAV) ratio has crossed below 1 for the first time, as the company's combined debt, preferred stock, and equity now exceed the value of its bitcoin treasury. MSTR shares closed Thursday at [$82.31, a 52-week low](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mstr/), amid a sustained slide in both the stock and its preferred securities.

Strategy's [June 22 SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1050446/000119312526276717/0001193125-26-276717-index.htm) confirms it holds 847,363 BTC. At Friday's spot price near $60,500, that puts the bitcoin treasury at approximately $51.3 billion. The company's enterprise value, comprising roughly [$28.9 billion in equity market cap](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mstr/) plus $6.75 billion in convertible debt and $15.5 billion in preferred stock, came to an estimated $51.2 billion, pushing the enterprise mNAV to approximately 0.99x.

## Accretion Channel Closes

Strategy's model depends on issuing equity at a premium to NAV. When mNAV exceeds 1, each new share issued at the prevailing stock price buys more than a dollar of bitcoin, increasing bitcoin-per-share for existing holders. Strategy's [first-quarter earnings filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1050446/000105044626000024/0001050446-26-000024-index.htm) shows preferred dividend obligations reached $229.5 million in Q1 2026 alone, with the cumulative preferred stock outstanding growing rapidly as the company raised over $13.5 billion in preferred equity since early 2025.

Fortune [wrote in January](https://fortune.com/2026/01/02/michael-saylor-strategy-mnav-bitcoin/) that a sub-1 mNAV would represent a crisis: "the reason for holding the stock vanishes, and no one will be likely to provide the company with more capital." CEO Phong Le told a podcast [in December](https://fortune.com/2025/12/02/stocks-bitcoin-strategy-ratio-market-cap/) that Strategy would consider selling bitcoin if the ratio fell below 1 and other capital sources ran dry.

## STRC at Record Lows

STRC, Strategy's variable-rate Series A perpetual preferred, has fallen to around $75, about 25% below its $100 par value, [per market data](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/strc/history/). Strategy's [June 22 SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1050446/000119312526276717/0001193125-26-276717-index.htm) shows the company's USD Reserve stood at $1.4 billion as of June 21, 2026, a management-designated fund intended to cover preferred dividends and debt interest.

The Defiant has tracked the STRC decline through [record-low territory](https://thedefiant.io/strategy-strc-record-low-leverage-cascade-june-2026), a [Rosen Law Firm securities probe](https://thedefiant.io/strategy-securities-probe-mstr-two-year-low-strc-below-par), and [MSTR's break below $100](https://thedefiant.io/strategy-stock-falls-below-usd100-for-first-time-in-two-years-as-analysts-pick-apart-its-bitcoin) earlier this week.

For institutional observers of the corporate bitcoin treasury model, a sub-1 enterprise mNAV severs the equity-accretion channel that made the approach replicable. Other bitcoin treasury companies have faced similar compression: [CoinGecko's treasury tracker](https://www.coingecko.com/en/public-companies-bitcoin) shows Metaplanet's basic mNAV at 0.84x as of late June.
