# External Brief: mom_multiasset_r1

The research starts from mature public methods rather than the existing QQQ/TQQQ parameter neighborhood. Canonical cross-sectional momentum, time-series momentum, the 52-week-high effect and momentum-crash evidence define distinct hypotheses. Current MSCI and S&P methodologies show that investable momentum commonly blends six- and twelve-month returns, adjusts for volatility, limits concentration and controls turnover.

Recent evidence also changes the default assumption: a 2026 point-in-time, cost-aware study reports weak or negative results for a classic 12-1 implementation. Therefore 12-1 is a baseline to falsify, not the presumed champion. The matrix includes long-only risk-adjusted scores, trend quality, residual or sector-relative momentum, and explicit transaction-cost stress.

Universe construction is a first-class research problem. The round begins with a dated Nasdaq current-market snapshot, narrows through typed eligibility and Longbridge adjusted-history checks, and freezes the resulting universe for forward virtual paper. Historical tests on that current universe are labelled exploratory because the repository does not yet hold historical constituent membership.

ML enters only after deterministic survival. Qlib, RD-Agent and AlphaBench support a workflow in which broad, source-linked factors are compiled and evaluated systematically. Open Composer will use LLMs for research and typed factor proposals, deterministic code for feature calculation, and grouped chronological validation for model rejection. No LLM call participates in live routing.
