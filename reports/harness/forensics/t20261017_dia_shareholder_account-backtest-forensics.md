This is a post-result-selected implementation study on seen dates. Six fixed policy/unit candidates generate twelve separately funded cost paths and six immutable connected contexts. Whole-share and cash-funding effects do not create an independent time sample or repair the original four uncertain comparisons. Cash distributions are not adjusted-return DRIP; joint close fills and conservative payment credits are conditional conventions. PBO and DSR are unavailable rather than fabricated. Rights and source completeness limit point identification where actually held. No deployment or new trial is authorized.
