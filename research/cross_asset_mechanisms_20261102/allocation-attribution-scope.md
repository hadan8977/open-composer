# A4 allocation attribution: proposed follow-on scope

Investment question: does A4's monthly allocation add costed account value over
holding a comparable amount of the unchanged D1 sleeve beside unchanged S3?
The alternative explanation is a favorable average D1 allocation, not useful
month-to-month risk adaptation. This finite task must separate these explanations
and show the return sacrificed relative to the higher-risk practical S3/A1 books.

This is a branch of the same direction and cumulative ledger, not a reset. The
preceding accepted scope is `scope.md`; its raw output is under
`runs/mechanisms-20261102-1/worker/evidence/`. Read the independent Lead reception
at `runs/mechanisms-20261102-1/lead/review.zh.md` and `lead/raw-audit.json` first.
Preserve the old plan failures, both numerical batches, receipt repair and source

## Why this question now

The completed comparison contains 24 listed configurations, 23 distinct new
mechanisms and 52 known cumulative starts including failed publications and four
ancestor candidates. A4's $20k account adds about $7,557/$7,376 at 10/20bp versus
an ex-post same-volatility S3/SHY control. Its 10bp paired 95% interval includes
zero. M1 and W4 have narrower, positive conditional intervals and remain useful
counterevidence, not rejected parent mechanisms. Pure S3 makes more dollars at
much higher volatility/drawdown. A4's D1/S3 allocation is the remaining mechanism
to explain before adding another momentum or cap search.

The original D1 K1 pass and K2 stress Sharpe edge +0.037 below +0.05 remain frozen.
Original sources/code are listed in `scope.md`, including Carlson source cards,

## Freeze before computation

Fresh independent plan review is required for the new matching/control definitions
and two allocation interventions. This proposal does not self-approve them.
After that review, one worker prepares, implements, computes, repairs and interprets
the entire package. Reuse accepted unchanged inputs, tests and contracts; no
per-variant review, additional reviewer chain or general test suite.

Use exactly the saved sanitized close/open matrices, source D1 and source S3
signals, 63-session costed standalone volatility, 25%-75% cap, monthly offset zero,
and actual combined asset target/event semantics. D1/S3 targets are netted before
charging account orders. Old holdings earn the overnight move; a completed close
can first change holdings at the next open. Any combined rebalance restores both
sleeve targets; between events the share positions drift. All new controls use
the same event union as A4, including source S3 changes and monthly decisions.

Primary data remain 2023-09-18..2025-12-31, 575 seen development sessions. Every
book starts at $20,000 and includes initial entry costs. Independently calculate
10bp and 20bp per side on actual netted orders and native residual-cash financing.
For cost-responsive allocation rules, use the corresponding costed standalone
histories in each scenario and disclose that both fees and small target changes
can affect the difference. Reuse the original fixed 25/50/75% books where their
full definitions agree; do not approximate dollar accounts by blending returns.

Freeze these nonselectable controls before looking at new account outcomes:

1. **Same-risk fixed D1 share.** Hold a constant D1 share a and S3 share 1-a,
   restoring targets on the common A4 event schedule. Separately at each cost,
   solve a in [0.25,0.75] for development volatility equal to that cost's A4 book,
   with annual-volatility absolute tolerance 1e-6. Use deterministic bracketing
   and the existing shared account cache. This is an ex-post risk-normalization
   control, not a tradable fitted allocation. If bracketing/matching fails or
   multiple solutions matter, report unmatched evidence and the risk gaps;
   do not introduce leverage or select the most favorable root.
2. **Same-average fixed share.** Hold the arithmetic mean of A4's prescribed D1
   share effective on the development session opens. Compute that mean from the
   known close-target history shifted to its effective open, with no weighting
   by subsequent returns. This is another ex-post descriptive control. Report
   its remaining volatility gap rather than calling it same-risk.
3. **Pre-window fixed share.** Freeze the D1 share that A4 could set at the final
   close before development, for the whole development period. This is causal;
   reuse a matching existing fixed-allocation account if equivalent. Show risk
   gaps, because equal initial information does not imply equal realized risk.

Two distinct, prespecified causal interventions test whether current monthly
risk adaptation is informative. Count them as selectable starts in the existing
ledger even if the task ultimately uses them only as counterevidence:

- L1: use the clipped A4 allocation from the preceding monthly decision at each
  current monthly decision. Use 50/50 before a prior valid decision exists.
- H1: at each monthly decision use the expanding arithmetic mean of clipped A4
  allocations from earlier monthly decisions with valid positive 63-session
  volatility for both sleeves. Include all such dates in the saved prehistory,
  exclude the current decision, and use 50/50 if that set is empty. Hold this
  allocation until the next monthly decision. This removes rapid adaptation
  while retaining a causal estimate of the historical allocation level.

Each intervention uses unchanged underlying signals, the common event schedule,
and full costed accounts. Compare it with A4, the practical fixed controls and its
own ex-post same-risk fixed-D1 control under the above matching rule. A control
root solve or bootstrap draw is not a new economic variant. Any further mechanism
requires an explicit economic reason; do not append parameter grids or select
allocation lookbacks/caps on these results.
