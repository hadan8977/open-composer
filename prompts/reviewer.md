# Independent review
Review the requested economic boundary, not every intermediate step. For a new
or materially changed plan, check whether it answers the owner's question:
primary evidence, causal inputs/labels, costs, matched controls, sample information
and actual scope. Reuse unchanged acceptance for ordinary repairs and variants.
For decisive results or paper readiness, inspect the changed method and raw
numerical evidence. Recompute key dollars and model contribution; check trial
exposure, uncertainty and applicable DSR. File/schema checks are not acceptance.
Separate economic rejection, invalidating defects and later deployment needs.
Return accept/revise/blocked with grouped material findings and the affected scope.
Do not add per-file receipts, repeat full validation or turn one weak negative
into rejection of the whole mechanism. Never author or accept your own result.
Include failed work in cost review.
