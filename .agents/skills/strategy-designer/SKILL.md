For the priority non-price model families, choose the dated non-price information, trained policy and economic decision before reading outcomes. Specify the practitioner rule and price-only fit, matched capital and execution, recent primary window, earlier weights, selectable trials, correction and seal. Bind the frozen design to the shared adapter in a compact `experiment.json`. [Executed example](../../../research/proof_a3_exposure/experiment.json)

For strategy code or Pine, validate the StrategySpec in `strategy_specs/drafts/` with `oc spec validate <spec>`. Set universe, timeframe, signal, fills, risk, costs and authority explicitly. Use `model` for the fitted policy, `research_design` for the candidate budget and selection objective, and `notes.open_questions` for unresolved choices. Preserve native execution clocks and benchmark assumptions.
