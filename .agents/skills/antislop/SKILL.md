# Antislop

Check whether the design's choices serve its content, users, and identity. Use the [local skill map](../impeccable/reference/skill-map.md) for scope. Broad design work is led by impeccable; this skill supplies a focused filter.

## Infer the job from the request

- During authorized design work, apply the checks to the choices being made.
- For an audit, report findings without editing.
- For a fix request, correct the relevant causes while preserving the selected direction.

Do not ask a separate "during or after" question when the request already establishes the mode. Reading this skill does not start an installation wizard, create project instructions, or require a report file.

## Integrity and function

Keep product claims, data, testimonials, people, and capability descriptions grounded in supplied evidence. Clearly label illustrative content. No invented social proof or decorative charts presented as real data.

An interactive control has a real action and honest feedback. Preserve actual pending, failure, success, empty, and recovery states where applicable. Keep navigation destinations valid, keyboard operation and focus visible, and content usable at the relevant screen sizes.

These are functional requirements. A particular visual technique is not a substitute for satisfying them.

## Purpose test

For a questionable major choice, identify what it does for hierarchy, reading, comparison, navigation, feedback, or product identity. A user's explicit visual direction is a legitimate reason.

Watch for clusters of interchangeable choices: a repeated hero/card template, decorative labels with no information, identical effects across unrelated controls, generic claims, fake data, or motion that competes with the task.

Fonts, gradients, blue or purple, glass, rounded shapes, cards, icons, conventional layouts, and punctuation are not intrinsically defects. Look at their use and effect. A stable tool may benefit from predictable repetition; a showcase may benefit from expressive composition.

Ask whether the product would remain recognizable through its content organization, typography, and interaction if the logo disappeared. If not, return to the specific brief and references rather than adding a stock accent.

## Preserve life and clarity

Removing decoration does not complete a design. Keep the selected concept's distinctive composition, rhythm, tone, imagery, or interaction. Do not replace every criticized style with the same beige editorial layout, dark dashboard, or neutral gray screen.

Check the assembled interface. Several restrained layers can still form a heavy frame; removing a wrapper must preserve alignment, touch targets, readable labels, and focus.

Use [avoid-ai-design](../avoid-ai-design/SKILL.md) for a detailed UI diagnosis, [uncodixfy](../uncodixfy/SKILL.md) for simplifying task UI, or [no-ai-slop](../no-ai-slop/SKILL.md) for a separate prose-editing task. Do not run all of them as consecutive gates.

## Finish

Report only concrete findings and the rationale for consequential changes. Cite the affected element, task, and observed consequence; label unrendered judgments as provisional. Preserve successful choices. Stop when the requested work and relevant verification are complete, without a mandatory all-rules pass/fail report.
