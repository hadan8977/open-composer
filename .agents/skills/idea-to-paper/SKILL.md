# From an idea to an economic decision

Identify the decision the idea should improve and its contribution to the existing portfolio or research. A component study need not create a standalone strategy, account, or sleeve. Answer a narrow challenge directly when the existing evidence suffices.

Use [the mission](../../../docs/research-mission.zh.md) for priorities, [research workflow](../../../research/README.md) for continuity and method changes, and [the review standard](../../../docs/research-plan-review-standard.zh.md) for scientific acceptance. These are the shared policies; do not reproduce them as another workflow.

## Frame the experiment

For a new direction or material design, inspect original papers, implementations, public results, independent replications, changes since publication, and counterevidence. Retain fetched evidence; search snippets are not source snapshots. Reuse fresh local findings and distinguish faithful replication from local adaptation.

Choose the cheapest informative test that can change the decision. Match sample, information, model capacity, and execution scale to the claim. Nearby parameter or cost variants do not establish mechanism coverage. Apply baselines, placebos, power and multiplicity analysis to the actual risk. Account for borrow, financing, fees, slippage, latency, permanent security identity, and price hygiene where relevant. Small-account whole-share limits and IEX/SIP differences can change implementation economics; paper fills do not prove live executability.

Register adjustable candidates, selection rules and cumulative trials through the existing contracts. Design model roles, matched incremental-value checks, missing-modality tests and deterministic fallback for their intended use. Preserve prior failures and exposure when new evidence justifies reopening a question.

## Use the applicable native path

Use [research-plan-reviewer](../research-plan-reviewer/SKILL.md) for new economic hypotheses, material scientific definitions or genuinely new scope. Use [strategy-research-orchestrator](../strategy-research-orchestrator/SKILL.md) for strategy-specific tools and gates; source preparation and repairs do not authorize dependent economic computation before those gates pass.

Deliver the economic conclusion, raw evidence, limits, cumulative investment, unresolved claim and next falsifiable action through the existing task and reporting route. Distinguish stopping allocation from refuting a mechanism; `not_at_depth` does not grant more resources. Report performance as `N/A` without a valid evaluation.

For forward or paper work, consult the existing sleeve contracts in [the v3 plan](../../../docs/plan-v3-2026-10-06.zh.md), [evaluation standards](../../../docs/evaluation-standard.zh.md), and [owner reporting](../../../docs/owner-reporting.zh.md). Cleanup does not cancel their applicable 60-trading-day validity, interactive authorization, IEX signal comparisons, monitoring or stop rules.
